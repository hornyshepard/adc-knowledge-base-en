## 4. Taxonomy feedback

- **Law and privacy** (GDPR, consent, claims, influencer disclosure) have no skill of their own; I put them in DIG and SOC. They would deserve an "Ethics and law in advertising" section (possibly in ADV or CLI), because they cut across skills.
- **Media planning and media buying** (payment models, budget, frequency, programmatic, ad fraud, brand safety) are split between ADV ("basic media planning") and DIG; the border is unclear. I put the digital part in DIG and the budget in ADV.
- **UX / digital experience** (Morville's honeycomb, the laws of UX) sits between DIG, EXP and ART.
- **Dynamic / modular creative** (dynamic banners, responsive ads) is on the border between ART, CPY, DIG and AIM; it deserves a cross-cutting map in the next phase.
