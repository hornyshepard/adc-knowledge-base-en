### PIT (pitch-presentation)
- PIT-1401: Cut-through appears where emotion, simplicity and energy meet
- PIT-1402: A pitch is built in six steps: Discover, Distil, Design, Drill, Deliver, Debrief
- PIT-1403: Before any pitch, define the "exam question" and decide whether it is worth taking part
- PIT-1404: The audience communicates from four energy centers: head, heart, gut and feet
- PIT-1405: Audience research separates verifiable facts from feelings
- PIT-1406: The objective of a pitch is visualized, felt and only then stated in under ten words
- PIT-1407: The intention of a presentation answers four questions: Know, Feel, Do and Why now
- PIT-1408: A pitch is organized like a tree: one headline, three key messages and the evidence underneath
- PIT-1409: The audience's emotional journey is planned through active verbs
- PIT-1410: The ask in a pitch is the "logical next step", not the final "yes"
- PIT-1411: The urgency of a pitch is built on "towards" or "away from" motivation
- PIT-1412: "Stardust": five types of evidence that make a message memorable
- PIT-1413: The blockbuster formula: 20% setup, 70% confrontation, 10% resolution
- PIT-1414: The S.T.A.R. opening: Surprise, Takeaways, Authority, Roadmap
- PIT-1415: The middle of the presentation is organized in three parts: POP, PPF and ten other formulas
- PIT-1416: The stories in a pitch are structured with SELL or SOLVE, and the hero is the client
- PIT-1417: The S.A.L.T. close controls the last impression, according to the peak-end rule
- PIT-1418: Slides are scenery, not a script: six principles of deck design
- PIT-1419: Present from frameworks and keywords, not from a memorized script
- PIT-1420: Rehearsal goes through five stages, from "the car crash" to the dress rehearsal
- PIT-1421: The pitch team is chosen by ABC and judged by its transitions
- PIT-1422: Q&A is prepared in five steps, and the answer is given with the P + + – P formula
- PIT-1423: Objections are handled in five steps: Reveal, Relate, Refine, Reduce, Redirect
- PIT-1424: Nerves are energy: reframing, "quick wins" and breathing
- PIT-1425: The speaker leads the room's energy at "level 8" and warms up body and voice
- PIT-1426: A virtual presentation calls for the thinking of a TV editor
- PIT-1427: Involving the audience: rhetorical questions, interaction frames and names
- PIT-1428: The debrief within 24 hours, the presentation bank and the learning log
- PIT-1429: Every presentation has competition: Define, Differentiate, Destroy
- PIT-1430: Saying why you are the right one is an act of generosity toward the audience
- PIT-1431: Rehearsing on video: a single flaw corrected per round (SRC-044)
- PIT-1432: Debate: stage fright: calming techniques (Colenso) vs. removing the unknown through video (Nishad)

### PRT (portfolio)
- PRT-1401: The portfolio is a product, and its user is the person who hires
- PRT-1402: The portfolio sent in advance ≠ the portfolio presentation
- PRT-1403: Portfolio stories are collected during the projects (an indexed journal)
- PRT-1404: Curation starts from analyzing the job ad (Portfolio Planner)
- PRT-1405: Two or three excellent projects beat five surrounded by mediocrity
- PRT-1406: The portfolio is designed for 30 seconds: fast track / slow track
- PRT-1407: A customizable PDF first, then a site; portfolio communities are risky
- PRT-1408: The introduction and the headings sell relevance, not adjectives (the X-Y-Z formula)
