## 4. Feedback on the taxonomy

- **Customer experience and retail** (omnichannel, the store as an experience, IRL spaces) are split between DIG and EXP. A clarification in the scope would help: EXP for experience design, DIG for channels and data.
- **Marketing metrics** (PAR/BAR, ROMI, CLV) have no clear home: I put them in ADV (measuring effectiveness) and AIM (predictive CLV). A "measurement" subsection in ADV would help.
- **Organizational capabilities** (digital readiness, agile marketing, silos, employee experience) do not fit well in any skill; I put them in MKT and DIG. They could go into CLI ("the business of the agency and the client").
- **Metaverse and Web3** sit between SOC (future social media), DIG and AIM; I put them in SOC, following the book's thesis (the metaverse as the next step in the evolution of social media).
- **Social marketing, sustainability and emerging markets** (bottom of the pyramid, the social enterprise) fit in MKT, but they are a large enough subfield for a separate tag or chapter.
- **Employer branding / internal marketing** (BRD-0204) goes in BRD, but the BRD scope does not mention it.
