## 4. Feedback on the taxonomy

- **Corporate (not marketing) strategy**: business and operating models, governance, execution (SRC-007, ch. 5–7) have no skill of their own. I put them in MKT, but part of it (Operating Model 4.0, the six delivery phases) has no natural place in any ADC category. I skipped it on purpose.
- **Futurology and reading sources critically**: MKT-0319 is in fact a cross-cutting skill (*media literacy* for strategists). It would deserve either a map in `30-maps/` or a mention in the MKT or INS scope ("how to evaluate a source / a trend report").
- **Sustainable marketing and greenwashing**: fits in MKT (the scope mentions it), but it is just as much an ADV/CPY topic (communicating environmental claims) and ART (packaging). A cross-cutting "Responsible communication" map (MKT-0320–0324, BRD-0302, ADV-0302, BRD-0301, AIM-0301) would be useful.
- **Packaging design** does not appear in the ART scope. MKT-0324 has `also: art-direction`, but ART could explicitly include "packaging / circular design".
- **Ethics and representation in advertising** (ADV-0302): there is no clear place. It is either ADV or a subtopic shared with AIM (data ethics). It might be worth adding to the ADV scope.
- **Internal debate within the same source** (MKT-0312): the `debate` type also works well for tensions inside the same book, not only between authors.

- SRC-007 is a very visual PDF. The text extraction mixes in diagram labels. I filtered out the short lines to read the flow, then reread the key sections in the raw text (pp. 185–196, 211–219, 253–255, 302–308). The pages cited are the PDF pages, as in the `.toc.txt` file.
- For SRC-009 I did not find the author in the text. I used the attribution from the brief ("The DX Review").
- I did not use links to the hubs `[[MKT]]` etc., because the `SKILL.md` files do not exist yet and would have shown up as broken links.
