### BRF (20)

- BRF-0701: The creative brief is the architect's plan for the execution: a map for the creative work and a criterion for evaluation *(kind: concept, level 1)*
- BRF-0702: The McTigue–Rucker brief has six components and fits on a single page *(kind: framework, level 1)*
- BRF-0703: The objective in the brief is a communication objective: specific, falsifiable and tied to the main barrier *(kind: principle, level 2)*
- BRF-0704: Targeting means prioritizing: three typical errors and the Own–Attract–Accept target *(kind: framework, level 2)*
- BRF-0705: The positioning statement in the brief: target, frame of reference, point of difference, reason to believe *(kind: framework, level 1)*
- BRF-0706: The benefit ladder climbs from attributes to emotional benefits, and the chosen benefit must pass four tests *(kind: framework, level 2)*
- BRF-0707: One benefit, not five: an overloaded positioning blocks the creative idea *(kind: principle, level 1)*
- BRF-0708: Debate: the emotional benefit is not automatically superior to the functional one *(kind: debate, level 3)*
- BRF-0709: The execution section of the brief has four pillars: personality, mandatories, timing and budget, media *(kind: framework, level 2)*
- BRF-0710: Measurement is written into the brief before the campaign, and testing is justified by uncertainty and stakes *(kind: principle, level 2)*
- BRF-0711: Feedback on creative work is organized in four categories: love, like, questions, red flags *(kind: method, level 2)*
- BRF-0712: ADPLAN: six questions for an execution that is "on brief" but can still fail *(kind: checklist, level 2)*
- BRF-0713: The brief "fences in the yard": the strategic constraint gives creatives a place to start *(kind: principle, level 1)*

- BRF-0715: The Euro RSCG Australia brief model: the task, three triangulated insights, the creative work and a chain of approvals *(kind: checklist, level 2)*
- BRF-0716: The Get/To/By brief puts the whole strategy in one sentence with four links: target, response, message, reason *(kind: checklist, level 1)*
- BRF-0717: Naked Brief: an "informative and inspirational" brief, built on context, learning and three types of measures *(kind: checklist, level 2)*
- BRF-0718: The Red Cell brief: eight requirements, "execution hints" and an approval calendar on a single page *(kind: checklist, level 2)*
- BRF-0719: The Singleton Ogilvy & Mather brief: the role of communication, a useful insight, think/feel/do and the single point with its proof *(kind: checklist, level 2)*
- BRF-0720: Agency brief models share the same core, but each optimizes something else: inspiration, concision, context, process or proof *(kind: framework, level 2)*
