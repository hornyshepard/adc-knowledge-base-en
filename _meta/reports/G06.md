## 4. Feedback on the taxonomy

- **Corporate, internal and crisis communication** (ADV-0621, ADV-0622) have no clear home: I put them in ADV, but they would suit a PR / corporate communication skill, which is missing.
- **Advertising production** (shooting, casting, pre-production, production budget, Milavsky ch. 9) has no skill; it would fall between ART, CLI and a possible "production" skill.
- **Media planning** is mentioned in the ADV scope, but it probably deserves a separate skill or map (GRP, reach, pool rotation, audiences).
- **Communication theories** (Jakobson, semiotics, Barthes) ended up in CPY; a "semiotics and communication" skill or map would group them better with ART.
- **Advertising ethics and regulation** (ARPP, laws, greenwashing, anchor prices) have no home; right now they are only mentioned in the sources and in ADV-0622.
