### MKT: marketing-fundamentals (29)
- MKT-0101: Marketing creates, captures, communicates and delivers value (the AMA definition)
- MKT-0102: Value is what you get relative to what you give, not a low price
- MKT-0103: Marketing evolved from a production orientation to sales, market and value orientations
- MKT-0104: The 4P marketing mix: product creates, price captures, place delivers, promotion communicates value
- MKT-0105: A customer is worth the relationship, not the transaction: customer lifetime value (CLV)
- MKT-0106: Conscious marketing puts a higher purpose at the center of the business; CSR only adds it at the margin (*debate*)
- MKT-0107: Marketing strategy = target market + mix + a sustainable competitive advantage, built like a wall
- MKT-0108: The marketing plan has five steps grouped in three phases (with the critique of the BCG matrix)
- MKT-0109: The SWOT analysis separates what belongs to the firm from what belongs to the market
- MKT-0110: The marketing environment: the immediate environment and the CDSTEP macroenvironment (the Grewal-Levy version of PESTEL)
- MKT-0111: Four paths to growth: current or new market × current or new product (the Ansoff matrix)
- MKT-0112: STP is done in five steps
- MKT-0113: Demographics and geography help you find the target; benefits and lifestyle help you persuade it
- MKT-0114: A segment must pass five criteria; then you choose one of four targeting strategies
- MKT-0115: Positioning is built on value, attributes, symbols or the competition, and checked on the perceptual map
- MKT-0116: The value proposition: the needs–offer overlap with no competitor, written in four elements (the positioning statement)
- MKT-0117: A product has three layers (and the Kotler version in five levels)
- MKT-0118: The product life cycle and the self-fulfilling prophecy trap
- MKT-0119: An innovation spreads through five adopter groups (Rogers)
- MKT-0120: Price is the customer's total sacrifice and is set through the 5Cs
- MKT-0121: EDLP or high/low; skimming or penetration (plus psychological tactics)
- MKT-0122: Distribution: direct or indirect channel, three levels of intensity
- MKT-0123: Services are intangible, inseparable, heterogeneous, perishable
- MKT-0124: Four service gaps, and the communication gap is created by advertising that overpromises
- MKT-0125: Marketing research in five steps; data become information
- MKT-0126: Secondary and primary data, qualitative and quantitative, questionnaire mistakes, the 5Vs of big data
- MKT-0127: The selling concept vs. the marketing concept (five management philosophies)
- MKT-0128: For services, the mix becomes the 7Ps
- MKT-0129: Social marketing sells behaviors, not products

### INS: consumer-insight (9)
- INS-0101: The purchase decision has five stages and continues after the purchase (satisfaction, dissonance, loyalty)
- INS-0102: Every product meets functional and psychological needs, in different proportions
- INS-0103: Five types of perceived risk hold back the purchase
- INS-0104: The consumer chooses from a small evoked set, by the determinant attribute
- INS-0105: Choice architecture: nudges and default options
- INS-0106: An attitude has three components: cognitive, affective, behavioral
- INS-0107: Involvement decides central or peripheral processing (ELM)
- INS-0108: Psychological, social and situational factors, and the marketing mix
- INS-0109: Four types of buying behavior (the Assael matrix)

### BRD: brand-strategy (5)
- BRD-0101: The brand shortens the customer's decision and protects the firm from price competition (brand elements)
- BRD-0102: Brand equity: awareness, perceived value, associations, loyalty
- BRD-0103: Who owns the brand and how you name it (manufacturer or retailer; family or individual)
- BRD-0104: A brand extension saves on awareness but can dilute the brand (four rules)
- BRD-0105: Repositioning: Old Spice, CoverGirl, WW (*case*)

### ADV: advertising-strategy (10)
- ADV-0101: IMC treats all channels as one whole
- ADV-0102: AIDA: think, feel, do, with a lagged effect
- ADV-0103: The budget from objectives and tasks; efficiency through GRP, CTR and ROMI
- ADV-0104: The advertising campaign in seven steps (incl. the anatomy of a print ad and pre/post-testing)
- ADV-0105: An ad informs, persuades or reminds, depending on the product's stage
- ADV-0106: The message (a unique, relevant, sustainable USP) and the informational or emotional appeal
- ADV-0107: The media mix: mass or niche; continuous scheduling, flighting, pulsing
- ADV-0108: Puffery is legal, a number must be proven (and consumer rights)
- ADV-0109: PR earns credibility because the space is not bought
- ADV-0110: Promotions bring sales today but can steal from tomorrow's

## 4. Feedback on the taxonomy

- **Price, distribution, services, marketing research and B2B** all fit in MKT, which becomes very large (29 notes from G01 alone). Sub-maps in `30-maps/` might be worth it (for example "The marketing mix" and "Research and data").
- **Regulation, ethics and advertising law** (puffery, consumer rights, influencer disclosure, GDPR/opt-in) have no skill of their own. I put them in ADV/MKT/SOC. An "Ethics and regulation" skill or map would be useful, especially for the EU and Romanian context.
- **Social marketing and public interest campaigns** sit between MKT, ADV and STO.
- **Sensory factors and retail atmosphere** sit between INS and EXP.
- **Marketing research** (methods, questionnaires) is split between MKT and INS. The INS scope mentions "consumer research"; it should be made clear where the method notes go.
