# Agent reports

The reports of phase 1 (`G01–G15`) and phase 2 (`P2-*`), plus the merge lists (`merges-*.txt`). They are historical: the IDs in them are **temporary** (e.g. `BRD-0503`). The mapping to the final IDs is in `_meta/renumber-map.json`; the merges that were applied are in `_meta/merges.log`.
