## 4. Feedback on the taxonomy

- **Advertising ethics** (manipulation, misleading inferences, children and materialism, greenwashing, femvertising, subliminal) has no skill of its own; right now it is scattered across INS, CPY, ADV, BRD. AIM covers only AI ethics. It would deserve either an "Ethics and responsibility in communication" skill or a cross-cutting map in `30-maps/`.
- **Research methods and measuring effectiveness** (experiment vs. survey, mediation/moderation, recall vs. attitude, implicit measures, projective techniques, the replication crisis) are only partly in INS and ADV. A "Research and effectiveness" map would help.
- **Consumer Culture Theory / the critical perspective** (postmodernism, myths, gender, politics, globalization) fits poorly in INS (psychologically oriented); I used INS, BRD, MKT, SOC, EXP. Grobalization/glocalization (Ritzer) and place branding (Relph, Belfast) from SRC-013 were not noted: candidates for BRD/MKT in the next phase.
- **Research-based art direction** (gaze, lateralization, visual salience): a single ART note; a "visuals and attention" subsection in ART would be useful.
