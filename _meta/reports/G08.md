## Taxonomy feedback

- **Advertising ethics and regulation** have no skill of their own. I put these notes in HIS (HIS-0806–0808, 0817, 0818, 0824, 0825), but they are just as relevant to CLI and ADV. A cross-cutting "Ethics and law in advertising" map (30-maps) would help.
- **Media planning** (the history of media, outdoor, radio, TV, direct mail) has no clear place: ADV covers only "basic media planning", DIG only digital. For that reason I left out much of Richards ch. 13–15 (media technology).
- **Political advertising and social advertising** (HIS-0809, HIS-0822) fit poorly in the existing skills; they would deserve a shared tag or a map.
- **PR** (Ivy Lee, Bernays, Arthur Page, from ch. 10) has no skill; I did not create dedicated notes.
- The lessons on the creative process (IDE-0802, CPY-0807) sit on the IDE/CPY border; the IDE scope could explicitly mention "the creative process and how the team is organized".
