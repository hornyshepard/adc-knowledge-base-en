# Translation from Romanian to English (30 Oct 2026)

This English edition was translated from the Romanian original (hornyshepard/adc-knowledge-base, commit c646854). Every note was checked against the English text of its books in `90-library/text/`: terms, model names, examples and quotes follow the books' own wording. Each note keeps at most one verbatim quote under 15 words.

IDs, typed links and the link graph are unchanged (7,732 links, same sources, targets and relation types). File names now come from the English titles. Relation words: fundament → foundation, extinde → extends, aplicare → application, exemplu → example, contrazice → contradicts, alternativa → alternative, vezi → see.

Where the Romanian note disagreed with the book, the English note follows the book. Misspelled proper names in the books (people, brands) use the correct spelling. Locators for SRC-019 use the book's own label, "outil N". The list below is the record kept per batch: what changed against the Romanian, and what could not be confirmed in the book text (kept, without quote marks).
