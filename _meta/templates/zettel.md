# The title is a precise claim or concept

> **The idea in one sentence:** the essence of the note, worded so that it can be true or false.

## Explanation

The idea explained in your own words, with the book's own terms, in 1–3 paragraphs. What it says, why it matters, where it comes from (the author / the model). If it is a model, a table with its components.

## Application

- What a strategist / copywriter / art director / student concretely does with this idea.
- The working steps or questions.

## Example

A real case (brand, campaign) from the source or from advertising practice, briefly.

## Questions

- A check question or a question for seminar discussion.
- Where does the idea NOT apply? Which author would contradict it?
