# The Future of Marketing: Harnessing Artificial Intelligence for Success

**Chidiebere Iroegbu, 2023.** Self-published; the author presents himself as a digital marketing consultant. Foreword by Collins Chukwu Richard ("AI Specialist"). About 9,500 words, 15 chapters of 1–2 pages each.

## At a glance

- **What it is about:** a list of topics on AI in marketing (data, personalization, chatbots, predictive analytics, content, SEO, programmatic, social media, influencers, ethics, upskilling marketers, implementation by company size, "case studies", the future). Each topic gets a paragraph of two or three sentences.
- **Central thesis:** AI is a paradigm shift that calls for personalization, data-driven decisions, ethics and continuous learning. Human-AI collaboration is augmentation, not replacement.
- **For whom and when:** an annotated table of contents. At most a checklist of topics for students starting the AIM module.
- **Relevance for ADC:** ★☆☆☆☆: no methods, no concrete examples, no data. The only elements kept: the criteria for evaluating influencers with AI (audience authenticity, fit, reputation, micro-influencers, transparency) and the remark about "filter bubbles" as a social effect of over-personalization.

## What is missing / what to read critically

- **Very low information density:** each subheading has two or three general sentences. The book is the length of a long article.
- **The ten "case studies" are not cases:** "a global e-commerce giant", "a telecommunications company", "a cosmetics brand", with no names, figures, years or sources.
- The foreword speaks of "the authors" in the plural, although the book has a single author. It is a sign of a template text.
- Unsupported claims about precision ("surgical precision") and about the future, with no discussion of the limits (attribution, incrementality, data quality).
- It brings nothing specific to creative work or brand strategy.
