# Facebook Marketing & Facebook Ads Beginner To Pro

**Bharat Bhuval Nishad, 2024.** Self-published (cover, design and editing by the same author). The name in the text is "Bharat Bhuval Nishad", not "Bhushan". The "About" section promotes a video course by someone else ("Facebook Marketing & Facebook Ads Mastery Course by Ahmed Amr"), and the spoken style ("Hey there, fellas", "in this lesson") and the transcription errors show that the text looks like a transcript of a video course. The real author of the content is therefore uncertain.

## At a glance

- **What it is about:** about 16,000 words in 20 short lessons: creating the page, "optimised" posts, promoting posts, groups, a content calendar, Facebook Live, giveaways for leads, a sign-up form with Mailchimp, a web traffic ad, remarketing with a pixel, a marketing plan, tactics for local businesses, contest ideas, trends, do's and don'ts, tools, "success stories".
- **Central thesis:** on Facebook you first build an organic presence and a relationship with the audience, then accelerate with ads, lead capture and remarketing.
- **For whom and when:** complete beginners, small entrepreneurs; useful in a seminar only as a starting point for critical discussion and as a list of tactics.
- **Relevance for ADC:** ★★☆☆☆ (2): the basic tactics are correct and in a logical order, but the book is shallow, dated and has no sources.

## What is missing / what to read critically

- The interface described is dated: old Ads Manager objectives ("Traffic", "Conversions"), page tabs, "Facebook Pixel" instead of Meta Pixel.
- Figures without sources: the "best" posting times, "two posts per day", a tool said to guarantee a "five hundred and ten percent increase in engagement", "success stories" without references.
- No discussion of consent, GDPR or the platform's rules for contests, although it recommends pixels, giveaways and data capture.
- Promotional claims (every strategy is supposedly "future proof", Facebook is supposedly "the best platform for marketing").
- The originality and the author of the content are uncertain (it looks like the transcript of a course).
