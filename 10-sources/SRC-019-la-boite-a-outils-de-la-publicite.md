# La boîte à outils de la publicité

**Servanne Barre, Anne-Marie Gayrard-Carrera, 2015.** Dunod, Paris, "La boîte à outils" series. The authors teach at Sup de Pub (a French communication school); one tool (*outil*) is written with Boris Langlois (Publicis Conseil), dossier 3 with Bénédicte Monville de Cecco, dossier 7 with Jacques Régnier. About 70,000 words, 192 pages, 62 tools in 9 dossiers. The text extracted from the PDF has OCR noise (watermark, some boxes lost, pp. 149 and 151 blank).

## At a glance

- **What it is about:** a handbook of tools, with a double-page sheet per tool (summary, "why", "how", tips, example), covering the whole cycle of advertising work: the brief, planning, the strategic communication plan, communication theories, the behavior of targets, creative work, 360° media and digital, measuring effectiveness, the legal environment and the jobs in an agency.
- **Central thesis:** the advertising idea remains the same alchemy, but the way it is rolled out changes: the agency justifies its value through its panoramic view and the coherence of a complete system (media, non-media, digital, content), built step by step from a well-posed communication problem.
- **For whom and when:** students in years 1–2, as a "route" through the communication plan and as professional vocabulary (in the French tradition: the copy strategy (*copy strat*), the recommendation (*reco*), the core target (*cœur de cible*), brakes and motivations (*freins et motivations*)). Very good for seminar assignments where students build a complete recommendation.
- **Relevance for ADC:** ★★★★☆. Extremely practical and well structured, with ready-to-use checklists. Limits: data and examples that are strictly French and from 2013–2014 (Médiamétrie statistics, French laws, platforms such as Viadeo, Google+, Vine); digital is described at its 2014 level, and the sheets are sometimes superficial. Creativity itself (how the idea is found) is missing.

## What is missing / what to read critically

- The market data (audiences, numbers of users, investments) are French and from 2013–2014; do not use them as they are.
- Dossier 6 (channels) is the most perishable: platforms that have disappeared or become marginal (Google+, Viadeo, Vine, TumblR), mobile described before vertical video.
- The communication models are presented in a textbook way (Jakobson, Maslow, the cognitive–affective–conative hierarchy), without the critical discussion of the recent literature (for example, the fact that many purchases do not follow the order "I know → I like → I buy").
- The creative process (how ideas are generated and evaluated) is almost entirely missing. "Disruption" and "storytelling" are two-page sheets.
- Dossier 8 (law, ARPP, Loi Sapin) is useful only for the French context; for Romania it has to be replaced with the local framework (CNA, RAC, EU legislation).
