# Agency brief models (Euro RSCG, Get/To/By, Naked, Red Cell, Singleton Ogilvy & Mather)

**Five real brief forms, undated and with no individual author.** Files: `brief-eurorscg-australia.txt` (a one-page form), `brief-get-to-by-brief.txt` (four lines), `brief-naked-brief.txt` (two pages, with instructions), `brief-red-cell-creative-brief.txt` (one page, "Str. 1/1", internal file "Creative brief TRC.doc"), `brief-singleton-ogivlyandmather-creative-brief.txt` (transcribed by hand from an image).

## At a glance

- **What it is about:** the blank forms that agencies use to pass the strategy on to the creative teams. They are not argumentative texts; their "philosophy" is read from what they ask, in what order and what they leave out.
- **Central thesis (implicit):** all of them converge on the same core (role/objective, audience, insight, a single proposition, proof, tone, mandatories), but each one optimizes something else: inspiration, brevity, context, process or proof.
- **For whom and when:** the practical BRF seminar, after [[SRC-022-the-creative-brief-blueprint|SRC-022]]: students fill in the same case in two different forms and compare the results.
- **Relevance for ADC:** ★★★★★ (5). These are the real tools they will work with; limit: the filled-in versions and the user guides are missing (except for Naked).

## Key ideas → permanent notes

- [[BRF-017-the-euro-rscg-australia-brief-model-the-task-three|BRF-017]]: Euro RSCG triangulates the insight (consumer, product/brand, category) and bases the creative "on the insights above".
- [[BRF-016-the-get-to-by-brief-puts-the-whole-strategy-into-one|BRF-016]]: Get/To/By compresses the strategy into four links: target, desired response, message, reason why.
- [[BRF-018-naked-brief-an-informative-and-inspirational-brief-built-on|BRF-018]]: Naked asks for an "informative and inspirational" brief, with history and learning.
- [[BRF-019-the-red-cell-brief-eight-requirements-execution-hints-and-a|BRF-019]]: Red Cell puts the emphasis on process: an internal timetable, five signatures.
- [[BRF-020-the-singleton-ogilvy-mather-brief-the-role-of-the|BRF-020]]: Singleton Ogilvy & Mather ties the single point to "emotional, sensual or rational" evidence.
- [[BRF-021-agency-brief-models-share-the-same-core-but-each-one|BRF-021]]: the comparison and what each one optimizes.
- [[BRF-012-one-benefit-not-five-an-overpacked-position-blocks-the|BRF-012]]: the convergence on a single proposition.

## What is missing / what to read critically

- The date, the context of use and filled-in examples are missing; we do not know whether the forms are still in use (the Euro RSCG network became Havas Worldwide, and Singleton Ogilvy & Mather seems to have been folded into the Ogilvy network later).
- The Singleton Ogilvy & Mather form is a manual transcription from an image: the page layout (for example the two columns "single point | evidence") is reconstructed.
- The "Str. 1/1" mark and the abbreviations SD/SP, AD/AE, PD on the Red Cell form suggest a local office in a non-English-speaking country; the reading of the abbreviations is ours.
- Only two forms (Euro RSCG, Naked) have a measurement heading; none explicitly asks for the competitive frame of reference.

## Quote
