# The experience unfolds in four stages (pre-consumption, purchase, core, remembered) and each one is designed separately

> **The idea in one sentence:** the experience does not start at the till and does not end at the exit; it has four stages (Arnould & Price), and the marketer can act on each one with different levers.

## Explanation

Arnould & Price (1993) describe four stages of the consumption experience:
1. **Pre-consumption experience**: searching for, planning, day-dreaming, imagining the experience;
2. **Purchase experience**: choice, payment, packaging, interaction with salespeople, service, environment;
3. **Core experience**: sensation, satiety, satisfaction or irritation, flow, transformation;
4. **Remembered experience**: nostalgia, reliving the past experience through photographs.

Lemon & Verhoef (2016) see the customer journey as an iterative process, prepurchase → purchase → postpurchase, with touchpoints of which only some are controlled by the firm. Batat faults them for leaving out perception levels, the difference between physical and digital touchpoints, and a clear distinction between what the firm controls and what it does not. Her definition: the journey is a continuum of personal responses to any interaction, at the micro, meso and macro levels. The firm never fully controls the journey; it can only anticipate the functional, symbolic and emotional needs.

## Application

The mini-case on wildlife-watching tourism (ch. 4) gives a practical guide by stage:

| Stage | Levers |
|---|---|
| Before the purchase | videos, photos, webcams, forecasts, reviews; text that engages the senses and creates emotion |
| After booking, before the experience | preparation without killing off the surprise: in Cairns, reef talks of about two hours before the dive, plus plasticized cards listing the species; films during the waiting time |
| During the experience | the guide (personality, knowledge) stresses the rarity, signals the best photo opportunities, involves visitors in spotting animals, keeps surprises (dolphins are not announced) and retreats to let them take in the moment |
| After the experience | a summary of what was seen; a photo sent by e-mail; an invitation to the community and to rating sites |

For a brand activation: write an emotional objective and a concrete gesture for each stage.

## Example

The queue at the entrance of the Coca-Cola pavilion at the Shanghai World Expo was entertained by bottle-shaped screens: the pre-consumption stage was designed as carefully as the inside.
