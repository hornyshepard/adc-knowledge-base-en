# Case: "Badoit Express" links a mineral water to gastronomy through a surprise dinner on the Paris subway

> **The idea in one sentence:** to associate its water with fine French cuisine, Badoit turned an ordinary subway ride into a gastronomic restaurant for 400 passengers, and the press amplified the gesture.

## Explanation

Badoit is a naturally carbonated mineral water from Saint-Galmier. Batat uses the case as an example of **taste marketing**, the most difficult of the five senses to apply, for which most food and beverage brands limit themselves to free tastings in the store.

In 2013, the brand launched the "Badoit Express" operation with the gastronomic chef Thierry Marx and the French rail company SNCF. A Paris subway train was turned into a restaurant: a gastronomic menu (pressed veal, lentils with foie gras, lamb with vegetables, snow eggs for dessert), served with Badoit. The roughly 400 stunned passengers ate on the way to their destination, and the press relayed the story. The brand's marketing director, Guillaume Millet, explained the idea by the brand's historic link with the French culinary heritage, at everyday and at exceptional meals.

Batat describes the operation as a "disruptive approach" that enchants customers in their daily routine (in the terms of the ETM matrix, enchantment).

## Application

Why it works and what you can borrow:
- **Association, not demonstration.** Water has no spectacular taste; the taste comes from the food it is paired with. The brand borrows the values of gastronomy.
- **Contrast.** An ordinary, hurried setting (the subway) makes the experience memorable; the message would have been weak in a restaurant.
- **Credible partners.** A well-known chef gives legitimacy, and the transport operator makes the execution possible.
- **Small reach, big echo.** The direct experience reaches hundreds of people; the story reaches the public through the press and social media. Design "how it will be retold" from the start.

## Example

An idea from the same family: the "Sprite shower" on the beaches of Rio, shaped like a giant soda fountain, which turns "refreshment" into a physical sensation where the public needs it.

## Questions

- Batat offers no impact data (sales, awareness). How would you evaluate the campaign beyond media coverage?
- What should the brand do after the event so that the association with gastronomy does not remain a single story?
