# Experiential marketing (EXP)

The skill covers the customer experience (CX) and its most visible part for an agency: activations, events, brand spaces and the bridges between the physical and the digital. The backbone is Wided Batat's book (*Experiential Marketing*, the 7E mix); *Marketing 6.0* brings the newer view of multisensory experience, the third place and the hybrid experience; the digital textbook by Zahay et al., Kotler 4.0, Wuebben, Parsons et al. and Kellogg complete the definitions and the cases. Note: Batat's own frameworks (7E, ETM, EXQUAL) are not empirically validated, and most of the cases have no measured results.

## What mastering this skill means

Observable competencies ("you can…"):

- **C1** You can explain the difference between customer experience, experiential marketing and a simple activation, and why experience is a distinct economic offering (Pine & Gilmore).
- **C2** You can diagnose a brand's experience with the drivers-markers-outcomes framework and with the 7E mix, separating what the company controls from what it can only anticipate.
- **C3** You can design an activation, a pop-up or a stand by stages (pre-consumption → remembered experience), with immersion, a theme, coherent sensory signatures and a memorable ending.
- **C4** You can choose the human-digital balance and the right phygital tools for a store or an omnichannel journey, starting from the customer's frustrations, not from the technology.
- **C5** You can run an "emotional touchpoints" workshop and build the journey map from customers' stories (the emic perspective), not from the company's assumptions.
- **C6** You can propose how to measure an experience beyond satisfaction: memorability, experience quality, the optimal level of service.
- **C7** You can argue, with cases, where spectacle helps and where the "enriched ordinary" or the brand stepping back is the better choice.

## How an AI agent uses this skill

1. **When you are asked for an activation, a pop-up, a stand or an event:** start from [[EXP-001-experience-is-a-distinct-economic-offering-the-progression|EXP-001]] (which realms you cover) and [[EXP-003-the-experience-unfolds-in-four-stages-pre-consumption|EXP-003]] (write one emotional objective and one gesture for each stage). Build the setting with the checklist from [[EXP-008-immersion-is-the-way-into-the-experience-five-factors-in|EXP-008]], the theme from [[EXP-009-theming-unifies-the-decor-and-turns-an-ordinary-offering|EXP-009]] and 1–2 sensory signatures from [[EXP-010-sensory-marketing-gives-the-brand-a-signature-for-each|EXP-010]]. Add a moment of surprise ([[EXP-011-the-wow-moment-is-surprising-personal-and-contagious-and-it|EXP-011]]) and something the audience *does* ([[EXP-012-storyliving-brands-that-make-their-story-lived-by-the|EXP-012]]). Check with [[EXP-013-a-memorable-experience-does-not-necessarily-require-the|EXP-013]] where you step back. Use [[EXP-030-case-the-coca-cola-pavilion-at-the-shanghai-world-expo-2010|EXP-030]] and [[EXP-031-case-badoit-express-links-a-mineral-water-to-gastronomy|EXP-031]] as reference points and [[SOC-046-fyre-festival-and-other-failures-what-happens-when-the|SOC-046]] as a warning.
2. **When you audit a brand's experience:** define the scope with [[EXP-002-customer-experience-is-the-lived-sum-of-all-interactions|EXP-002]] (the Temkin grid on the "boring" touchpoints), diagnose with [[EXP-004-customer-experience-is-a-process-with-drivers-markers-and|EXP-004]] and [[EXP-005-customer-experience-has-five-markers-holistic-cumulative|EXP-005]], then run the brand through the 7E grid from [[EXP-007-the-experiential-marketing-mix-7es-replaces-the-7ps-with|EXP-007]]. For qualitative data, propose the workshop from [[EXP-020-emotional-touchpoint-cards-a-six-step-workshop-to-find-out|EXP-020]] ([[EXP-019-emotional-touchpoints-a-touchpoint-is-defined-by-the|EXP-019]]) and a map built from customers' stories ([[EXP-021-the-emic-etic-process-the-experience-is-designed-by-looking|EXP-021]]). Place the experiences in the matrix from [[EXP-014-the-experience-territory-matrix-crosses-the-customer-s|EXP-014]].
3. **When you work on a store, a flagship or an omnichannel journey:** ask what social reason the customer has to come ([[EXP-022-physical-space-survives-as-a-third-place-a-social-place|EXP-022]]), decide the human-digital scenario for each touchpoint ([[EXP-024-how-much-of-the-experience-digital-can-take-over-depends-on|EXP-024]]), choose the phygital tools by the moment they serve ([[EXP-025-the-phygital-experience-links-the-store-and-digital-without|EXP-025]], [[EXP-026-phygital-experiences-come-in-six-typologies-connected-web|EXP-026]]) and check the handoffs to the courier, the installer or the platform ([[EXP-017-extension-the-experience-must-be-designed-as-a-continuum|EXP-017]], [[EXP-027-for-direct-to-consumer-brands-the-brand-is-the-complete|EXP-027]]). For a launch that has to be memorable, design the threshold as in a rite ([[EXP-023-a-memorable-retail-experience-can-be-built-as-a-rite-of|EXP-023]]).
4. **When you need to justify the budget or measure an experience:** define the target outcome with [[EXP-006-a-successful-experience-produces-four-outcomes-value|EXP-006]] (including the memorability question), propose the dimensions from [[EXP-028-experience-quality-is-measured-beyond-satisfaction-exqual|EXP-028]] and, for service packages, the logic from [[EXP-029-conjoint-service-optimization-shows-where-you-overdeliver|EXP-029]]. Say openly that the sources give few measured results and that media coverage is not an effect on the brand.
5. **When you write or evaluate a brief for an experience:** state the objectives on 2–3 Es from [[EXP-007-the-experiential-marketing-mix-7es-replaces-the-7ps-with|EXP-007]], specify the level of participation required ([[EXP-016-collaborative-marketing-has-three-levels-of-involvement-diy|EXP-016]], [[EXP-015-exchange-in-the-experiential-mix-price-gives-way-to-value|EXP-015]]) and how the experience will be told after the event ([[EXP-012-storyliving-brands-that-make-their-story-lived-by-the|EXP-012]], [[EXP-031-case-badoit-express-links-a-mineral-water-to-gastronomy|EXP-031]]).
