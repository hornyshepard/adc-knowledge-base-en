# Social media and influencer marketing (SOC)

The skill covers two related things: how a brand builds a social presence that serves people, not only the brand, and how it works with creators and influencers without just buying followers. The notes come mainly from Glenister (*Influencer Marketing Strategy*), Mueller and Rajaram (*Social Media Storytelling*), Zahay et al. (*Digital Marketing*) and from the digital textbooks of group 10 (Sachdev, Schwarz-Musch et al., Nishad).

## What mastering this skill means

Observable competencies ("you can…"):

- **C1** You can build a social media strategy in the order people → objectives → strategy → platform, and tie each objective to its KPI.
- **C2** You can choose the platform and the format (feed, stories, toks, live, group) by persona, the platform's role in the journey and the logic of the algorithms.
- **C3** You can tell a community from an audience and write a moderation guide that separates legitimate critics from trolls, plus a crisis protocol.
- **C4** You can draw up a shortlist of influencers on criteria of relevance, resonance, shared values and a verified audience, not by follower count.
- **C5** You can structure a collaboration with a creator: mission, open brief, fair compensation, usage rights, exclusivity and compliant #ad disclosure.
- **C6** You can read a social or influencer report critically: you separate vanity metrics from business metrics and calculate ER, CPE, CPM and ROI.
- **C7** You can argue, with cases, when long partnerships, ambassador programs, B2B influence or paid amplification of creator content are worth it.
