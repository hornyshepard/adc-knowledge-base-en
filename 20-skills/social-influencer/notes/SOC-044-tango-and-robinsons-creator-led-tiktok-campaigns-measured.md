# Tango and Robinsons: creator-led TikTok campaigns measured by brand lift

> **The idea in one sentence:** three Britvic campaigns made with the creator company Whalar show a repeatable pattern: one platform chosen for the audience, several creators with different styles, a simple idea interpreted freely and measurement through brand lift, not just views.

## Explanation

**Tango Editions Berry Peachy (2022).** Objective: fast awareness for a limited-edition flavor among Gen Z (16–24-year-olds), an audience that, according to the study cited, often spends zero hours a day on ad-supported TV and trusts creators' product recommendations (63% in the US, 58% in the UK). Strategy: TikTok, six creators, each with their own take on the drink's bold effect. The standout video (an undercover cop trying to blend in with the youth through a cringey use of slang) got 2.1 million organic views. In total: 10.6 million views, +7.5% awareness, +17 NPS points (nearly 3x the UK TikTok benchmark), +2.3% intent to purchase, the category's no. 1 launch of 2022.

**Tango × The Prince's Trust.** A custom sound, Hype Man (a voice feeding encouragement to the creators), over which the creators told their stories of needing a confidence boost; plus PR and an experiential activation. 8.9 million views, a 9.61% average view engagement rate against a 5.6% food and beverage benchmark.

**Robinsons Guess My Gargle.** A TikTok AR filter following a TV ad with a gargling choir: users gargle a song or a sound and challenge others to guess it. 22 creators from three categories (Fantastic Family Funnies, Making the Little'uns Laugh, Extra-Mile Exceptionals). Over 1,300 pieces of UGC, 77% brand recall (against a 71% benchmark), +9% top-of-mind awareness.

## Application

- Choose a single simple mechanic (a flavor as metaphor, a sound, a filter) that each creator can interpret in their own style.
- Combine categories of creators for different audiences within the same family.
- Ask for a brand lift study from the start; views alone do not prove the effect.

## Example

The best-performing Robinsons videos included multiple people and matched the creator's usual style, a sign that the fit between creator and format matters more than production.

## Questions

- The figures come from the studies of the agency that ran the campaign; what independent checks would you ask for?
- What role does Tango's advertising heritage ("You know when you've been Tango'd") play in its success on TikTok?
