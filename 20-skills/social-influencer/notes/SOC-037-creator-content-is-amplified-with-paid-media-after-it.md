# Creator content is amplified with paid media after it proves its performance

> **The idea in one sentence:** influencer content, like all content, is "hit or miss" and cannot be predicted, so the real value comes from monitoring each piece and amplifying with paid media the ones that perform, including beyond the creator's own audience.

## Explanation

Jim Tobin (Carusele), quoted by Glenister, names the common mistake: many brands believe that finding the right influencers is the key to success, but that is only the very first step. You need systems in place to monitor the performance of each piece of content and systems to amplify the high-performing pieces to the right target audience, even outside the influencer's own fan base. Continuous optimization (of the message, the content, the targeting and even the creators) will shape the future of the industry.

William Bonaddio recommends promoting creator content with paid media **from the influencer's own account**: for example, a micro-influencer who sings covers can be promoted to all her fans and to the fans of the artist whose song she is covering. Pieter Groenewald (Nfinity) observes that scaling smaller creators' content through performance media has become a widely adopted and cost-effective practice. Almost 90 percent of brands repurpose creator content on other channels, according to a survey cited, which also helps attribute the value of the campaign.

The principle has a contractual consequence: usage rights and permission to run ads from the creator's account must be negotiated in advance.

## Application

- Launch several pieces from several creators organically, read the first results and put the budget behind the ones that perform.
- Target the amplification at similar or adjacent audiences (the fans of an artist, of a niche).
- Budget the amplification separately from the creators' fees.

## Example

Coricraft, a South African furniture retailer, used existing customers as nano-influencers (10 creators, 43 pieces of UGC), then scaled the relatable content through performance media: 2.3× sales and a 6.9× ROAS reported. Volkswagen did something similar for the GTI launch with 12 nano-influencers and 9 micro-influencers.

## Questions

- Does "authentic" content lose credibility when it appears as a paid ad?
- How are disclosure responsibilities shared when the brand runs ads from the creator's account?
