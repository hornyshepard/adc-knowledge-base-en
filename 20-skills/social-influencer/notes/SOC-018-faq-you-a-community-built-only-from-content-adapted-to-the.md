# FAQ YOU: a community built only from content adapted to the platform

> **The idea in one sentence:** FAQ YOU, the sex education platform of the organization Youth Against AIDS, gathered more than 100,000 followers on TikTok within a few weeks without an advertising budget, without a dance challenge and without influencer collaborations: only with content adapted stylistically to the platform.

## Explanation

Maximilian Wolf, the organization's head of marketing, tells the story. For ten years, Youth Against AIDS had run peer-to-peer sex education workshops in schools. For the questions pupils asked most often, it published a book ("FAQ YOU", more than 30 authors, medical experts and social media personalities). The title became a brand, with its own channels.

What worked on TikTok:

- **One topic per post**, quick edits, building suspense, targeted use of text.
- **A tone of its own**: "loud, open and direct, but never pushy, indiscreet or unserious", answering questions about sex and love at eye level.
- **Timing**: among the first channels in TikTok's "Learning with TikTok" initiative. The first comments (viewers writing that their parents had been sitting next to them) disappeared within a few weeks, as the community got used to educational content.
- **Adapt the style, not the content**: the narrative stays the same, the form changes. On Instagram: memes and graphics as snackable content in the feed, IGTV for complicated topics ("project enlightenment: home edition" during the pandemic, then "69 seconds of sex-ed"), livestreams, its own 3D filters, the first "Guide" on body positivity.
- **A hub of its own**: a website that bundles all the content, moderated by a chatbot.

The users decide: the organization regularly evaluates new platforms and leaves the ones its audience has left. In the long run, the collaboration with the twins Heiko and Roman Lochmann (DieLochis), ambassadors since 2014, shows that the bond between people and message lasts longer than a one-off creator–product collaboration.

## Application

- A taboo subject can become a serial educational format if the tone suits the audience.
- Fix a message constant and let only the form and the format vary.
- Get in early on a new platform format or initiative: there is little competition.

## Example

The video that DieLochis released in 2015 for the organization's campaign "100 ways not to use a condom" passed one million views, because it was a perfect fit for the funny tone of their channel.

## Questions

- Wolf admits that high organic reach is getting harder to build; would the same strategy still work today without a budget?
- What can a commercial brand learn from an NGO that sells nothing?
