# Social media serves six distinct objectives, and each calls for a different KPI

> **The idea in one sentence:** "being active on social" is not an objective; the textbook distinguishes six goals (awareness, relationships and community, SEO, support and reputation, research, employee productivity), each with its own indicators.

## Explanation

| Objective | What it means | Typical KPIs |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | exposure through owned, earned and paid media | views, shares, new site visitors |
| **Relationships and community** | lasting connections, a brand community | followers, engagement, participation |
| **SEO** | indexed content (YouTube, Pinterest, Twitter) and links to the site | backlinks, organic traffic |
| **Support and reputation** | customers write on social first; you monitor what is said | response time and rate, sentiment |
| **Research** | social listening for trends, problems, new uses | insights, ideas implemented |
| **Productivity** | internal collaboration, employees as advocates | adoption, efficiency |

The **brand community** has a precise definition (Muniz and O'Guinn): a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on the social relations among admirers of a brand. It is not easy to build; sometimes, if you are lucky, it already exists and can be "gently coopted by the corporation." The Harley Owners Group had existed since the 1920s; Harley-Davidson assigned executives to go along on the rides before the internet, and today the local groups keep the community alive by themselves, with very few comments from the page administrators.

Social listening, with tools such as Meltwater or Salesforce Social Studio, serves both reputation and research.

## Application

- Choose one main objective per campaign and write its KPI and target (e.g., 5,000 new followers in three months).
- For ROI, link each post to the result through UTM parameters.
- Use social listening as a source of creative insight, not just for reporting.

## Example

By analyzing conversations about "robot vacuum", iRobot can learn that the robots get stuck or have too small dirt collection bins, but also that people name their Roomba and add stickers to it, like a pet: a good insight for advertising. My Starbucks Idea collected over 70,000 ideas in its first year; 277 were launched in five years (free wifi, free birthday treats).

## Questions

- How do you prove ROI for the community objective, which pays off over years?
- Should a brand community be moderated by the agency or by the client?
