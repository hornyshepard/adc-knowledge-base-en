# A social platform is chosen by persona, objective, message and its role on the path to purchase: not by fashion or total user numbers

> **The idea in one sentence:** each network has its own audience, role and language, and the right platform is the one where your persona is present and receptive to your message, not the one everyone is talking about.

## Explanation

Schwarz-Musch and his colleagues (SRC-032) put choosing the platform in second place, right after the persona, and test it with four questions, all of which must be answered "yes": (1) does my buyer persona use this platform? (2) can I achieve my goal with ads there? (3) is the platform suitable for my message? (4) is the persona receptive to my message on this platform?

Glenister (SRC-035) describes the same networks by their role on the path to purchase:

| Platform | Dominant role (Glenister) | Advertising specifics (Schwarz-Musch) |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | first for influencer marketing and direct purchase (Shopping, product tags) | the network of images; the ad has to look like valuable content |
| Facebook | the largest audience, growing among the over-65s; groups, live, contests | more than 3 billion users; B2C, awareness |
| YouTube | product research: reviews, unboxing, long video | skippable or non-skippable formats, bumpers |
| TikTok | discovery and entertainment; creators must be left free to experiment | young audience; the algorithm favors videos that are watched a lot |
| LinkedIn | B2B and thought leadership; personal profiles travel better than company pages | targeting by industry, job title, company size |
| X, Pinterest, Snapchat, Twitch | real-time conversation; inspiration and traffic to retail; young audience and comedy; live gaming | |

**Where they converge.** Schwarz-Musch, Glenister and Zahay ([[SOC-001-the-post-methodology-people-first-then-objectives-strategy|SOC-001]]) put the platform *after* people and objective. Adil Sbai (SRC-034) adds the creator's nuance: on Instagram the creator is in the foreground, on TikTok the content comes before the creator. **Where they differ.** Glenister thinks in roles along the journey, Schwarz-Musch in targeting capabilities; Glenister recommends small tests on several networks first, then focus.

## Application

- Build a persona × platform matrix and answer the four questions in each cell.
- Saga holidays for older people (Glenister) go on Facebook, not on Snapchat.
- In an influencer campaign, check whether the creator actually has a community on the chosen platform.

## Example

Schwarz-Musch compares a fashion brand for young adults aged 18 to 35 in major cities, which runs on Facebook and Instagram, with an enterprise software provider that targets IT managers in companies with more than 500 employees on LinkedIn.

## Questions

- How quickly does the table go out of date? What stays stable when platforms copy each other?
- What do you do when the persona is on a platform where your message is not welcome (financial services on TikTok)?
