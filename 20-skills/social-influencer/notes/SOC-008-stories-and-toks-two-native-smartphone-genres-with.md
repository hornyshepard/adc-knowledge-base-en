# Stories and toks: two native smartphone genres with different logics

> **The idea in one sentence:** stories (invented by Snapchat in 2013) and toks (TikTok's short videos) are the first native storytelling genres for smartphones, but they work differently: stories are a volatile diary for a community, toks are attention videos watched in their entirety.

## Explanation

Mueller and Rajaram note that feeds still translate old formats (articles, advertisements, galleries), while stories and toks fully exploit the smartphone: vertical 9:16, live or in real time, a shared social experience. The differences:

| | Stories | Toks |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | Snapchat, 2013; then on all platforms | TikTok (after the merger with Musical.ly, 2018); copied as Reels, Spotlight, Shorts |
| Structure | cards and clips of 10–15 s, strung together horizontally, deleted after 24 h | a short video with a timeline, effects, lots of music |
| Consumption | users swipe quickly back and forth; it is hard to measure how much they perceive | usually watched in their entirety; audio is indispensable |
| Logic | social interaction with a community that shares the same goals; can carry a lot of text; good for cross-promotion and CTAs | monetizable attention and recognition; memes, role-playing, makings-of, regional slang |
| Distribution | mostly to the community that already follows you | the algorithm first plays the post out to many accounts, then ranks it by their reactions; content is played out for weeks |

The TikTok algorithm "raises the bar", but gives every creator a fair chance at a high reach. On Instagram, stories have replaced the feed as the first destination for Generation Y and Gen Z. The authors also note that many successful TikTokers take their audience to long video on YouTube without explicit cross-promotion: people look them up on their own.

## Application

- Use stories for relationship and traffic (polls, behind the scenes, links, CTAs), toks for discovery and growth.
- Do not repost mechanically: a tok needs internal rhythm and audio, a story needs sequences planned card by card.
- Focus stories and toks on the story and the interaction, not on closing a sale.

## Example

CNN brings an everyday story about the onset of winter to Snapchat Stories with photos, short video clips, captions, reaction stickers and a location tag: a diary of the moment, not a commercial.

## Questions

- If every platform copies the same formats, does the original logic of each genre still matter?
- What does an algorithm that favors the content, not the account, mean for a big brand?
