# The 5Cs: people use social media to consume, connect, control, compete and create

> **The idea in one sentence:** a social campaign succeeds when it meets at least one of the five user motivations; the more it taps into, the better its chances.

## Explanation

The model belongs to Lauren Labrecque, a coauthor of the textbook, and is designed as an equivalent of the 4Ps for planning social content:

| C | The motivation | What the brand offers |
|---|---|---|
| **Consume** | information, entertainment, an escape from boredom | engaging, shareable content: how-tos, guides, stories |
| **Connect** | a connection with friends and with the brand | questions, polls, exclusive details, customer stories |
| **Control** | to influence the brand | voting on products, feedback, co-creating products and ads |
| **Compete** | to win, to be recognized | contests, giveaways, showcasing winners |
| **Create** | to produce things themselves | tools and incentives for user-generated content (UGC) |

The authors insist that digital is not one-to-many communication but two-way, which is exactly what Connect is about. Algorithms favor the content people interact with, so the 5Cs are also a reach tactic.

## Application

- When you assess a social idea, count how many Cs it taps; an idea with a single C (only Consume) needs more paid budget.
- Build the mechanics before the execution: what people can do (vote, recreate, compete), not just what they can watch.
- Give them tools: stickers, frames, templates, a hashtag; this lowers the effort of creating.

## Example

- **Consume:** Blendtec's "Will it Blend?": the blender blends iPhones; entertaining and informative, and later items were suggested by fans (nearly 13 million views for the iPhone video).
- **Connect:** #OREOscope on Twitter: a personalized horoscope based on the user's most recent tweets, easy to share.
- **Control + Compete:** Mountain Dew's "Dewmocracy", Lay's "Do Us a Flavor" (the winning flavor, Cheesy Garlic Bread, an 8 percent sales increase in three months), Doritos' "Crash the Superbowl", Lego Ideas.
- **Create:** the Getty Museum Challenge (works of art recreated at home during lockdown) and P&G's #DistanceDance with Charli D'Amelio (18.1 billion views on TikTok).

## Questions

- All the examples come from "fun" categories. How do you apply the 5Cs to a bank or an insurer?
- Where does Control become a risk (the brand loses control of its own product)?
