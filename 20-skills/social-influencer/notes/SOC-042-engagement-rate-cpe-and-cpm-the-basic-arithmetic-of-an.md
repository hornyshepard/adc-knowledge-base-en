# Engagement rate, CPE and CPM: the basic arithmetic of an influencer campaign

> **The idea in one sentence:** to compare creators and campaigns you need a few simple formulas (engagement rate, cost per engagement, cost per thousand impressions, cost per view) and platform benchmarks, always read against the objective.

## Explanation

Glenister gives the formulas and the practical benchmarks:

| Indicator | Calculation | Benchmarks from the book |
|---|---|---|
| **Engagement rate (ER)** | average engagements over the last 10 posts ÷ followers × 100 | average 3–6%; over 10% very good; on X 0.4–1% is good; over 5% excellent at selection |
| **Cost per engagement (CPE)** | cost ÷ engagements | about £0.21 as the fashion standard |
| **CPM** | cost ÷ impressions × 1,000 | £6 or below is good, about £10 is average (Mark Dandy) |
| **Cost per video view (CPV)** | cost ÷ views | on YouTube about £0.04–£0.10, higher in niches (travel, parenting, fitness) |
| **CPC** | cost ÷ clicks | n/a |

Worked examples: 20,000 engagements over 10 posts = 2,000 on average; at 100,000 followers, ER = 2%. Or: 1,500 likes + 250 comments + 100 clicks = 1,850 engagements at 40,000 followers, ER = 4.6%; for a $500 fee, CPE = $0.27.

Interpretation rules from the book:

- An account with 1 million followers is only likely to reach about 26% of that audience, which is why CPE is often more useful than CPM.
- A single campaign should have **one main objective**, from which the type of influencer and the KPIs follow.
- In the short term you measure engagement, reach and direct conversions (codes, traffic); in the long term perception and awareness (surveys or, more cheaply, social listening) and the value of repurposed content.
- Qualitative indicators: sentiment (positive ÷ positive + negative, watch out for irony and emojis), share of voice, *return on relationship* (Ted Rubin).

## Application

- Calculate the candidates' ER before negotiating, and use the target CPE to assess the fee asked.
- After the campaign, report which message, creator, format and time performed.
- Do not stop at the numbers: read the comments.

## Example

A leading UK fashion brand worked with 40 influencers (budget £15,000, average following about 174,000): an ER of 5.5% against a target of 2%, 381,073 engagements against 120,000, and a CPE of £0.04 against the £0.21 standard.

## Questions

- When is a high ER a sign of fraud (pods) rather than quality?
- Which indicator would you choose for an awareness campaign for a luxury brand?
