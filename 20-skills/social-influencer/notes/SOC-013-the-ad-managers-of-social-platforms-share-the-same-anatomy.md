# The ad managers of social platforms share the same anatomy: project goal, ad format, preview, target group and persona, duration, daily budget, result estimate, payment summary

> **The idea in one sentence:** the interfaces differ and change often, but the structure of the decisions stays the same; whoever learns it once can transfer it to any platform.

## Explanation

Schwarz-Musch and his colleagues show that almost all ad managers ask for the same decisions:

1. **The project goal**: at the macro level (brand awareness, interest) or the micro level (growing the community, interactions with posts).
2. **The ad format**: text, image, carousel, video, collection.
3. **The ad preview**.
4. **The target group and persona**: gender, age, location, interests, behaviors.
5. **The duration**: limited or continuous.
6. **The daily budget**, which may be exceeded slightly on a given day but balances out over time.
7. **The result estimate**: the possible reach and interactions.
8. **The payment summary**.
9. **Platform-specific elements**.

Examples of goals, which follow the journey: on Facebook, the automatic goal can be calls, website visitors, messages, Page likes or leads; the audience is defined by age from 13 to 65+, by location down to a radius of 1 km and by demographics, interests and behaviors. TikTok divides its objectives into awareness (reach), consideration (traffic, video views, community interaction) and conversions (app promotion, leads, website conversions).

Nishad (SRC-033) describes the same structure for a traffic ad, on three levels: campaign (the objective), ad set (audience, automatic or chosen placements, budget and schedule, a cost-per-click cap, without which the platform bids on its own and you may spend more than you planned) and ad (the page, the format, the image or video, the primary text, the headline, the URL, the CTA button).

## Application

- Make yourself a campaign sheet with the nine fields and fill it in before opening any platform.
- Tie the audience field to the minimum viable persona, not to intuition.

## Example

Facebook allows two kinds of ads: promoting a post already published on the page, so it reaches more people, and ads created separately, which do not appear on the company page; the same ad can run on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Audience Network.
