# Five micro-trends push toward immersive experiences: short-form video, community-based social media, interactive e-commerce, language-based AI and wearables

> **The idea in one sentence:** in each of the five components of marketing in a digital world (content, social media, e-commerce, AI, devices) a subtle shift is taking place, and they all point the same way: more interactive and more immersive.

## Explanation

*Marketing 6.0* identifies five micro-trends (Fig. 3.1):

| Component | Micro-trend | What it means for brands |
|---|---|---|
| **Content** | **short-form video** for *micro-moments* (Google and Boston Consulting Group: the impulsive moments when you pull out your phone to learn, do, discover or purchase) | short content, quick to produce, adapted to trends and, ideally, ***shoppable***: Target links the products directly from its Instagram videos |
| **Social media** | the move toward **community-based platforms** (Reddit, Discord, Mastodon, WhatsApp communities), out of concern over data privacy and a need for trust | young audiences want the **community**, not the algorithm, to decide what they see; the brand joins as a member, not as a broadcaster |
| **E-commerce** | **interactive** commerce: *social commerce* (you buy on the platform), *conversational commerce* (WhatsApp, Messenger, with human agents or chatbots), *livestream commerce* (from China) | product discovery becomes a real-time conversation |
| **AI** | **language-based AI**: voice assistants (around 43 percent of the US population), chatbots (74 percent favor them for basic inquiries, PSFK), ChatGPT, which reached 100 million users faster than any other technology product | AI becomes a conversation partner; it can draft personalized copy, summarize reports, analyze data |
| **Devices** | **immersive wearables**: 3D audio (Apple's Spatial Audio), smart glasses (Ray-Ban Stories, with AR) | digital content is consumed hands-free while you interact with the real world |

Generation Z increasingly uses YouTube, Instagram and TikTok as search engines. Morning Consult (2022) found that 98 percent of Generation Z in the United States used social media, and 71 percent used it for three hours or more daily.

## Application

- Plan campaigns around micro-moments: what does the person look for in 15 seconds, and what action do you offer right away (purchase, save, message)?
- For closed communities (Discord, groups), build value for members (access, exclusive content, conversation), not ads.
- Test conversational commerce: a well-written chat can be the brand's best salesperson.

## Example

The popularity of TikTok, with its short-form format and strong personalization algorithm, prompted Instagram and YouTube to launch Reels and Shorts, accommodating the selective attention of young users.

## Questions

- Community-based social media reduces paid reach. How do you measure success there?
- Livestream commerce works on a massive scale in China. What cultural barriers does it face in Europe?
