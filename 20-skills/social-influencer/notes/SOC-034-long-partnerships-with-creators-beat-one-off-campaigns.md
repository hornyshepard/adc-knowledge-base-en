# Long partnerships with creators beat one-off campaigns

> **The idea in one sentence:** repeated posts by the same creator about the same brand, over a long period, produce more trust and a better ROI than isolated collaborations, because the audience sees the product built into the creator's real life.

## Explanation

Both books converge here. Glenister states that brands and influencers are increasingly looking for longer-lasting "ambassador partnerships" and that regular posts from the same influencer have a higher ROI than one-off campaigns. Results show up within a few months, but consistency over about six months makes the difference (the example of Beats by Dre, which grew through influencers over time, not overnight). Davnet Doran (Britvic) describes the move from one-off spikes of activity to a steady pulse based on long-term relationships.

The streamer Mark Purdy (Valkia) explains the mechanism with his sponsor HyperX: the audience gradually warms up to the brand, sees constant and frequent use, and only then buys and shows him their purchases.

In *Social Media Storytelling*, Maximilian Wolf states the same idea for NGOs: do not think in terms of campaigns but of long-term cooperation, with explicit shared goals that weigh more than short-term KPIs; this gives the creator and their community time to make the organization's values their own.

## Application

- Propose always-on structures to clients, with a stable core of creators supplemented by tactical collaborations.
- Plan the relationship, not just the posts: briefs by stage, meetings, access to news, feedback.
- Also measure the cumulative effect (brand lift, consideration), not only each post.

## Example

Renault Scenic #BehindCarDoors: a year-long campaign with two parenting influencers (@mother_pukka and @father_of_daughters) about family life inside the car, with creative control left to the influencers. Reported results: over 2 million engagements, 80% of consumers felt the car seemed different from other cars, a 48% uplift in ad recall and a 15% uplift in consideration for the socially optimized creative, WhatCar searches up 80%, incremental sales revenue of about £860,000. The Lochmann twins were, from 2014, ambassadors of the Youth Against AIDS campaigns for over six years.

## Questions

- When does a long-term ambassador become "worn out" for the audience?
- What risks arise if the creator's values change during the partnership?
