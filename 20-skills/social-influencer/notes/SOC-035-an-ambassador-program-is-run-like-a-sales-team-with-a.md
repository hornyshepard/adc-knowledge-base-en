# An ambassador program is run like a sales team with a dedicated manager

> **The idea in one sentence:** an ambassador program turns influencers and passionate customers into a community of super-fans rewarded for referrals, but it works only with research, a clear framework, rewards that are easy to reach and a manager who looks after the people.

## Explanation

Glenister justifies the program by the power of referral: 84 percent of people buy a product based on a referral, and a referral-based sale can generate more profit (figures cited from SBI and Harvard Business Review). Mike Allton (Agorapulse) describes the philosophy: "You don't run campaigns, but build strategic relationships." He gives free access with no strings attached, and explores a collaboration only when he sees that the person is using and enjoying the product.

The proposed steps:

1. **Research**: what the community is called, what you expect from members, what rewards you offer, how you select them (personal invitation or open), how you promote the program, how you measure.
2. **Promotion**: in store, on the website, on social media, in the newsletter, in YouTube video descriptions; joining does not guarantee ambassador status; include loyal customers, not just influencers.
3. **Framework**: support materials without being too prescriptive (sample posts: close-up, lifestyle, description, CTA); a content plan with themed occasions (a day in my life, challenges, holidays, sales).
4. **Rewards**: at first, easy to reach (free products for simple actions), then variable commissions, bonuses, exclusive experiences, a points catalog, weekly competitions.
5. **Care**: regular contact, questions when performance drops, breaks accepted, member spotlights; Super Ambassadors who lead groups.
6. **Tracking**: average order value (it should be two to three times more than an average customer), lifetime value, program growth, referral sales, participation, feedback.

The suitable sectors have aspirational products (beauty, luxury, fashion, fitness, automotive, food and drink); an ambassador program for cleaning products, the author says, would not work.

## Application

- Treat ambassadors as a sales force with different motivations; study what the best ones do and share the lessons.
- Appoint a single program manager, available and responsive.
- Refresh the program periodically; no program lasts forever.

## Example

Glossier was launched from "Into the Gloss", Emily Weiss's beauty website and blog; the brand's ambassadors started as super-fans, and the growth came from brand stories that built trust.
