# The North Star idea is crystallized with the three Gs (goal, gap, gain) and tested with A.L.T.E.R.

> **The idea in one sentence:** a campaign's central idea shows where you want to go, which gap it fills and what people gain; then it has to attract, be timely, guide, engage and resonate.

## Explanation

Braun and Landa use the metaphor of the Pole Star: it is the only star that neither rises nor sets, so sailors steer by it. The North Star idea of a brand or a campaign is the central concept that keeps everything on a course aligned with the brand's principles, story and goals. It always starts from an insight into the audience.

**Crystallizing it: the three Gs** (Landa, *The New Art of Ideas*):

| G | The question |
|---|---|
| Goal | what do you want to achieve? |
| Gap | which unmet need, unexplored area, question not yet asked or underserved population does the idea address? |
| Gain | what benefit, functional or emotional, do people get? |

For Landa, the gap is the piece that differentiates the idea.

**The test: A.L.T.E.R.**

| Letter | Criterion |
|---|---|
| Attract | people notice it and find it appealing enough to talk about or share |
| Timely | it is opportune, well-timed, appropriate |
| Lodestar | it entails the central premise, mission and values; it guides the campaign in step with the company's actions |
| Engage | it is involving, stimulating, ideally participatory; it moves people to do something |
| Resonate | it is remarkable, relevant and responsive to people's aspirations |

The control question: would the idea make people think or feel something, change their point of view or their behavior?

## Application

- Write the three Gs for each concept on a single slide; if the gap is empty, the concept does not differentiate.
- Score the concepts on A.L.T.E.R. from 1 to 5; a weak score on Lodestar means a good idea for another brand.

## Example

Leo Burnett Australia for the insurer Suncorp: "One House to Save Many", a prototype house resilient to fire, flood, storm and cyclone. The goal was a new role for the brand; the gap, insurance that talks only about protection, not prevention; the gain, more resilient homes and communities. According to the book, the campaign is now also affecting Australia's building codes.
