# Brand strategy (BRD)

## What mastering this skill means

Observable competencies ("you can…"):

- **C1** You can explain what a brand is (a set of associations, a symbol, a collective myth) and how it creates value for the customer and for the firm, using brand equity as an audit grid.
- **C2** You can write a positioning statement (target, frame of reference, point of difference, reason to believe) and test it for clarity, credibility, distinctiveness and durability.
- **C3** You can choose the benefit and the relationship with the category leader (central or differentiated, user or product) with the expectancy-value model and the perceptual map.
- **C4** You can build a brand platform (mission, vision, values, purpose, personality or archetype) and ask for behavioral proof of each element.
- **C5** You can diagnose the brand's link with the customer's identity (motives, life transitions, community) and with the cultural tensions of the moment (Holt).
- **C6** You can decide the brand architecture, evaluate an extension and propose pruning a portfolio.
- **C7** You can create a new brand (hook, name, trademark, design) and plan its implementation: governance, consistency, services, global markets, digital.
- **C8** You can choose between sustaining the position and repositioning on the basis of brand health data, and argue the value of the brand in the language of finance, showing where the authors disagree.
