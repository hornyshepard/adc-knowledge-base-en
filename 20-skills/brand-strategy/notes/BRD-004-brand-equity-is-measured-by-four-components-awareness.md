# Brand equity is measured by four components: awareness, perceived value, associations and loyalty

> **The idea in one sentence:** brand equity is the set of assets and liabilities linked to a brand that add to or subtract from the value provided by the product, and experts assess it through brand awareness, perceived value, brand associations and brand loyalty.

## Explanation

Grewal and Levy define brand equity (an Aaker-type definition) as assets and liabilities linked to the brand, which a firm can build, manage and harness over time, like any physical possession. Four components:

| Component | Definition | How it is built / example |
|---|---|---|
| Brand awareness | how many consumers know the brand, know what it stands for and have an opinion about it | repeated exposures of the brand elements; it matters most for products bought without much thought and for infrequently purchased ones |
| Perceived value | the relationship between the product's benefits and its cost, compared with close competitors | Target's store brands (A New Day, Goodfellow & Co.): well-designed pieces at Target-level prices |
| Brand associations | the mental and emotional links between the brand and attributes, colors, slogans, personalities | the Prius: economical, stylish, good for the environment; the State Farm jingle from the 1970s, still in use |
| Brand loyalty | the consumer buys the same brand repeatedly rather than from multiple suppliers | lower marketing costs, free word of mouth, protection from competition |

A limiting case of awareness: some brands become **synonymous with the category** (Kleenex, Band-Aid, Google). For the brand, the risk is losing trademark status through generic use. For competitors, it is a barrier: who thinks of anything but Gatorade after working out? That is why WTRMLN WTR puts Beyoncé, an investor in the brand, up front.

The authors note that the benefits of brand equity are greater if the brand markets itself ethically, and that the value of a brand can be estimated as its earning potential over the next 12 months (Amazon, the most valuable brand in 2019, $315.5 billion).

Other sources look at the same notion from different angles. Bobby Calder (Kellogg) avoids the term "brand equity", which finance may misread as a financial term, and speaks of brand strength; Steven Struhl measures it through choices, as the premium people pay for the same features under a given name.

## Application

- A brand audit for a pitch can be structured around the four components: where is the brand strong and where is it vulnerable?
- Communication objectives can explicitly target one component: awareness (launch), associations (repositioning), loyalty (CRM).
- When you choose a jingle or a character, think about the associations over 20 years, not about a single campaign.

## Example

Lacoste can charge higher prices than other similar polo shirts, including retailers' own brands, because it is perceived to be of superior quality and gives status to those who wear it. Brand equity shows up directly in the price.

## Questions

- Is perceived value a component of brand equity or a consequence of the other three?
- How do you measure associations without qualitative research?
