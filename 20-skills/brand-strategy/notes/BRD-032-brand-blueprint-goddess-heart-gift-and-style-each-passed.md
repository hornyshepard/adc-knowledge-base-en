# Brand blueprint: goddess, heart, gift and style, each passed through a test

> **The idea in one sentence:** the brand platform in *Goddess Guide to Branding* has four layers (the archetype, or goddess; what you care about most, or heart; the feeling promised to customers, or gift; and the way you express yourself, or style), and each layer is chosen from several options through an explicit test.

## Explanation

McCarthy and McAndrew call *brand blueprint* what other strategists call brand identity or brand DNA:

| Layer | What it is | How it is phrased | The test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Goddess** | the archetype that inspires everything | a single goddess (or god) | authentic? attractive? |
| **Heart** | the passion that drives you, the "why" (with a reference to Simon Sinek) | a simple phrase with an active verb: "Inspiring Imagination" | is it genuine for the company? is it "big" enough for the goddess to care about it? |
| **Gift** | the emotion you leave people with, not the product | a feeling: trust, comfort, the feeling of being worthy | is it meaningful to the customer? is it special in the category? is it credible? |
| **Style** | everything that is seen, heard and experienced, including how the company operates | symbols and colors, voice and vocabulary, signature experience | is it unique? is it cohesive? |

Some tools from the chapters:
- For the heart, the exercise "what would your company never do?" brings out the core convictions.
- For the gift, the **Legend**: a Mad Libs-style story in which the customer is the heroine, the problem is the obstacle, and the brand appears as the guide who changes how she feels; and the **Brand Planet**, a drill down through layers: what you do → functional attributes → functional benefit → everyday emotions → deeper emotion. The deeper the feeling, the greater the power of the brand.
- For style: symbols and colors to own over the long term; 3–5 personality traits for the voice; lists of words always used and never used; signature experiences (product, packaging, team, partnerships, company policies).

The interview with Katie Conway adds a nuance: do not invest emotion in every touchpoint, but in two or three key moments, preferably where the category has a pain point (Apple's unboxing).

## Application

- Use the four layers as a brand platform structure for small brands or startups.
- Put the "heart" at the top of every brief, as the agency Global Prairie, quoted in the book, does.
- Use the Brand Planet as an insight exercise: stop at the deeper emotion and check whether the product can deliver it credibly.

## Example

Soft Scrubs Co.: the goddess Demeter; the heart "Caring for the caregivers"; the Brand Planet: makes scrubs → super soft, thick inner lining → warm → coziness → feeling comforted and held; the gift chosen: comfort; the style: the name Mother Scrubs, a heart symbol, heart red and oceanic turquoise, a supportive, warm voice, phrases such as "You Are Loved".
