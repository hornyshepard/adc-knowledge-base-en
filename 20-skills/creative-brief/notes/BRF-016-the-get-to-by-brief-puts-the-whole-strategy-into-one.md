# The Get/To/By brief puts the whole strategy into one sentence with four links: target, response, message, reason

> **The idea in one sentence:** if you cannot fill in "Get [target] to [think/feel/do] by telling [message] because [reason]", the strategy is not clear yet.

## Explanation

The form has only four lines with dotted rules:

| Link | What you fill in | Its counterpart in the McTigue and Rucker model |
|---|---|---|
| GET | the target | target |
| TO | what they must think, feel or do | communication objective (the desired response) |
| BY TELLING | the message | positioning, point of difference |
| BECAUSE | the reason (*reason why*) | reason to believe |

**The philosophy.** This is the brief reduced to its grammar. The formula forces three things that long forms can hide. (1) **Change**: the target is not a description but someone who has to move from one state to another. (2) **The distinction between response and message**: what you want the person to do is not the same as what you tell them so that they do it. (3) **Proof**: "because" calls for a verifiable reason, not an adjective. The "think/feel/do" version links the formula to the classic models of advertising effect, cognitive, affective and conative ([[ADV-007-any-communication-can-produce-four-effects-but-brand|ADV-007]]), without naming them; the same triad, with "why now" added, structures the intent of a pitch ([[PIT-003-a-presentation-s-intention-answers-four-questions-know-feel|PIT-003]]).

What is deliberately missing: the insight, the tone, the mandatories, the budget, the measurement. That is why Get/To/By works better as a **clarity test** or as a summary at the top of a full brief than as the only brief for a large production.

## Application

- Write the Get/To/By sentence before the full brief. If it does not come out, go back to the research.
- Use it as a one-line summary (like the "brief in brief" section of the Naked template, [[BRF-018-naked-brief-an-informative-and-inspirational-brief-built-on|BRF-018]]).
- As an exercise, ask each team member to write the sentence separately and compare: the differences show where there is no alignment.
- Check the TO link: is it a single response? "To learn about it, love it and buy it" are three objectives.

## Example

For Stark ([[BRF-015-the-stark-cloud-solutions-case-how-a-good-brief-becomes-a|BRF-015]]): *Get* the managers of SMBs with old servers *to believe* that their data are safer in the cloud than on their own server, *by telling* them that Stark protects better than any internal storage, *because* it uses industry-leading encryption.

## Questions

- Is the absence of the insight from the formula a weakness or a choice? Where would you write the insight?
- Does the formula fit ideas that are not messages (an activation, a useful service)? See [[STO-001-a-brand-story-is-shareworthy-when-it-has-use-value-for-the|STO-001]].
