# Content marketing strategy is a cycle: objectives and audience, creation, distribution and amplification, evaluation and a new round; Kotler breaks it into eight steps, Zahay into four

> **The idea in one sentence:** content marketing is not an isolated creative act but a cycle that starts from objectives and audience, produces or selects content, distributes and amplifies it, then measures and starts again; the authors differ mostly on the order of the steps and on the metrics.

## Explanation

**Kotler, Kartajaya and Setiawan (SRC-004)** give eight steps, grouped into pre-production, production, distribution and post-distribution: (1) goal setting, with sales-related or brand-related goals; (2) audience mapping, preferably of a subculture, with personas, anxieties and desires; (3) content ideation and planning: a theme tied to the brand's character, an episodic narrative; (4) content creation, as a publisher, or through outsourcing, sponsorship, UGC; (5) content distribution through owned, paid and earned media; (6) content amplification through influencers; (7) content marketing evaluation along the 5A path (visible, relatable, searchable, actionable, shareable); (8) content marketing improvement.

**Zahay and her coauthors (SRC-029)** give four steps: (1) **create**: produce content, **curate** it (locate and comment on other publishers' content, with a link and a citation) or buy **syndicated** content; Hootsuite's "Rule of Thirds": one third promotion, one third content from thought leaders, one third brand building; (2) **develop the audience**: build a community of loyal subscribers, not only convince people to buy; (3) **distribute** from a hub of your own, natively on each platform, in the order owned → earned → paid; (4) **evaluate** on four families of metrics: consumption, sharing, leads, sales.

**Where they converge and where they differ.** Both models close the loop with evaluation and a new round. Kotler puts objectives and audience before the idea; for Zahay the persona is a premise, but the cycle begins with creation. Kotler separates distribution from amplification through influencers; Zahay adds curation, the cheapest way to start. Barre and Gayrard-Carrera (SRC-019) offer a third version, in six steps, which also asks for a copy strategy for the content and a plan for consumer participation ([[STO-024-brand-content-reverses-the-direction-of-advertising|STO-024]]). Measurement remains the weak link: 79 percent of marketers agree that "My team makes awesome content that our audience loves", but only 18 percent understand its ROI.

## Application

- Build the plan on the four phases; an empty column (usually evaluation) shows an incomplete plan.
- Before production, tie each piece to a 5A stage and to a family of metrics.
- For a small client, start with professional curation.

## Example

Airbnb's "The Local List" (Kotler): guides to the favorite places of locals, for people who want to experience the city as a local. Hootsuite (Zahay) made curation a permanent feature: the "This Week in Social" blog post and a newsletter with the best posts of the week.
