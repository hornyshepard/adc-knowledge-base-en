# Six user needs guide the planning of social formats

> **The idea in one sentence:** people do not come to social media only for news; they meet six different needs, and each content format should explicitly serve one of them.

## Explanation

Mueller and Rajaram take up a model presented by Dmitry Shishkin (BBC) at the Google News Initiative in 2018. Users around the world look for six things in social stories:

| The need | What the user expects |
|---|---|
| **Educate me** | new skills or in-depth knowledge |
| **Entertain me** | everyday stories or originally produced content |
| **Inspire me** | stories about special people |
| **Distract me** | funny stories, memes |
| **Keep me informed** | the latest social media trends |
| **Keep me up to date** | the latest news |

The authors note that editors and media companies long overestimated the need to be updated, and focused everything on news. In reality, the same people use the platforms at different times of the day with different needs: a quick update in the morning, funny clips or short how-to videos at lunch, and at the weekend the long videos bookmarked during the week. The better you understand the habits of your community, the better the relationship and the stories work.

## Application

The grid becomes a tool for planning serial formats. The authors' example:

- *Educate me* → "one-minute learning" on TikTok;
- *Inspire me* → photo galleries with inspirational quotes in the Instagram feed;
- *Entertain me* → "behind the scenes" videos in the feed and Reels;
- *Inform me* → expert talks on Instagram Live / long video.

Check an editorial calendar: if all the posts serve the same need, you miss other moments of use.

## Example

FAQ YOU (Youth Against AIDS) combines *educate me* (short answers about sexuality on TikTok), *distract me* (memes in the Instagram feed) and *inform me* (long video on complicated topics), each on the right channel.

## Questions

- What need does a detergent brand serve? Can an "ordinary" brand own *educate me* or *inspire me*?
- Are there needs missing from the model (for example, belonging, status, the need to buy)?
