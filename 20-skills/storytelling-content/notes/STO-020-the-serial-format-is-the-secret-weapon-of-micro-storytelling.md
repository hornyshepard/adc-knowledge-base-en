# The serial format is the secret weapon of micro-storytelling

> **The idea in one sentence:** a serial format (regular content with a fixed structure, design and focus topic) lowers production effort, is easy to find in the feed and turns watching into a user habit.

## Explanation

Mueller and Rajaram call serial formats the "secret weapon" of micro-stories. Once a format has been tested and works, the storyteller no longer reinvents the structure: they focus on the story and introduce only small variations, which themselves work as story beats. The benefits are twofold:

- **For the creator:** time saved, a content template that fits the brand, easy to find in search and in the feed, and new episodes no longer have to be announced elaborately.
- **For the user:** they know what to expect (design, content, tone), come back on their own, build the series into their daily habits and are curious about the small deviations.

The authors advise that each serial format address a specific user need and be assigned to a specific platform. The variant of "serial theme days" (Monday, inspiration; Thursday, knowledge; Friday, live) also brings editorial planning security. A weekly live show at a fixed time serves both the "linear" audience, who come at the announced time, and the "non-linear" one, who watch the recording.

## Application

- Define for each series: name, intro and outro formula, length, platform, the need it serves.
- Examples of serial focus topics: "behind the scenes", "one-minute learning", the portrait of a colleague, the expert of the week.
- Leave room for playful deviations; without them, the series becomes monotonous.

## Example

Tim Hendrik Walter (@HerrAnwalt) reached more than two million followers on TikTok in a few months with "One Minute of Law": he takes up a legal question from a follower, introduces it through role play, says his intro formula with the questioner's handle, answers in exactly one minute and ends with "That was One Minute of Law". The "one-minute" format was then taken up by many other creators of learning content.
