# Storytelling and content (STO)

The skill has two halves that support each other: **the story** (how you build a narrative that moves people and deserves to be shared) and **the content** (how you turn stories into a permanent, planned, measured flow). The core sources are *Shareworthy* (Braun & Landa) for the advertising story, *Social Media Storytelling* (Mueller & Rajaram) for micro-stories, Zahay et al. and Richards & McLean for content strategy, plus Kotler, Sawhney (*Kellogg on Branding*) and Batat for the brand story.

## What mastering this skill means

Observable competencies ("you can…"):

- **C1** You can structure an ad story: protagonist, obstacle, wish, the brand's role and the transformation, in three acts, and you know when to break the structure.
- **C2** You can explain why the customer is the hero and the brand the guide, the mentor or the "oxygen", and where the authors disagree (social media, the founder as hero).
- **C3** You can check a story for emotion, authenticity, use-value and representation, with the checklists from *Shareworthy*.
- **C4** You can write micro-stories for social media: the anatomical moment, story beats, multimedia layers, templates and serial formats.
- **C5** You can build an always-on content strategy: point of mutuality, pillars, functions and stages, the production-distribution-evaluation cycle, repurposing (pyramid or all-in-one).
- **C6** You can design audience participation (UGC, challenge, co-creation) and you know what rights you need to reuse other people's content.
- **C7** You can evaluate a purpose campaign: purpose without washing, manifesto, reputational risk, stereotypes.
