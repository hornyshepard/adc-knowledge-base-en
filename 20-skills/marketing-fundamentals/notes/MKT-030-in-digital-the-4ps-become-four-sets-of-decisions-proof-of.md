# In digital, the 4Ps become four sets of decisions: proof of product value, pricing model, channel (omni-channel or disintermediation) and digital assets for promotion

> **The idea in one sentence:** Sachdev does not invent another mix; he turns each P into a list of questions specific to digital marketing.

## Explanation

| P | Questions for digital marketers (Exhibit 2-1) |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Are we demonstrating through digital means the value of our products and services? Are we providing value through digital services? Do we prolong the product's life cycle through upgrades? |
| **Price** | Which pricing strategy: subscription-based, flat-fee, bundled, freemium? Is the pricing clear, given that people compare prices easily online? |
| **Place** | What is the digital marketing channel? Do we pursue an omni-channel strategy (online and physical) or a disintermediation strategy (removing intermediaries)? |
| **Promotion** | Which platforms reach the target market? Do we create evergreen content? Do we use native ads? How do we spur digital word-of-mouth? Are we building digital assets? |

A few useful distinctions: **subscription-based** pricing (Netflix, with prices summarized simply), a **flat fee** per period, **bundled** pricing (home and automobile insurance in one price), **freemium** (a basic service at no charge, and you pay for added services or a premium level). **Digital assets** are all the digital items of value the organization owns: content, data, websites, accounts, communities. They take years to build, so they are capital, not a campaign expense.

Each digital P has to stay consistent with the organization's overall marketing objectives; in the strategic plan, the 4Ps sit alongside the 7As.

**Its place in the debate about the mix.** Sachdev is the most conservative of the authors in the library: he keeps the 4Ps and adds questions to them. Kotler translates them into the 4Cs (MKT-035), Wuebben replaces them with EP² (MKT-043), and Batat with the 7Es.

## Application

- Fill in the table for the client's brand before you propose a campaign; the answers on Price and Place often change the message.
- Treat content and community as long-term assets, with a maintenance budget.

## Example

Domino's AnyWare: pizza can be ordered through the website, the app, Google Home, Facebook Messenger or text message. The channel strategy (Place) becomes message and value in itself: you order in whatever way is most convenient for you. Peacock TV illustrates freemium: the free basic service has ads, Premium has limited ads, Plus is ad-free.

## Questions

- When does a pricing model (freemium, for instance) become the central idea of the communication?
- Which P is missing from a mix for purely digital services (people, process, physical evidence)?
