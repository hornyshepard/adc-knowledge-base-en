# The evolution of marketing from 1.0 to 6.0: from product to customer, to the human being, to digital, to technology for humanity and to immersion

> **The idea in one sentence:** the Kotler–Kartajaya–Setiawan series describes six stages of marketing, each triggered by a change in technology, generation or social context, and the stages do not replace one another but overlap.

## Explanation

| Stage (book) | Orientation | How the customer is seen | Driver | Notes to read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1.0** | product-centric | mass buyers with physical needs | Industrial Revolution; 1950s–1960s | 4P: [[MKT-020-the-4p-marketing-mix-product-creates-price-captures-place\|MKT-020]] |
| **2.0** | consumer-oriented | smarter consumer with mind and heart, but passive | information technology; 1970s–1980s | STP, positioning: [[MKT-014-stp-is-done-in-five-steps-strategy-segmentation\|MKT-014]] |
| **3.0** (2010) | values-driven | whole human with mind, heart and spirit | social media, mobile, open source; the 2008 crisis | [[MKT-033-marketing-3-0-treats-the-customer-as-a-whole-human-being\|MKT-033]], [[MKT-064-social-issues-are-addressed-in-three-stages-philanthropy\|MKT-064]], [[MKT-065-the-10-credos-of-marketing-3-0-integrate-values-into\|MKT-065]] |
| **4.0** (2017) | moving from traditional to digital | connected customer who decides socially | mobile, social media, connectivity | [[MKT-034-power-has-shifted-to-connected-customers-from-vertical\|MKT-034]], [[MKT-035-the-kotler-series-translates-the-classic-concepts-into\|MKT-035]], [[MKT-036-the-five-a-s-customer-path-aware-appeal-ask-act-advocate\|MKT-036]] |
| **5.0** (2021) | technology for humanity | segment of one, five generations, a polarized public | AI, NLP, sensors, IoT; the pandemic | [[MKT-038-marketing-5-0-applies-human-mimicking-technologies-across\|MKT-038]], [[MKT-040-prosperity-polarization-empties-the-middle-of-the-market\|MKT-040]], [[MKT-039-agile-marketing-executes-at-pace-and-scale-real-time\|MKT-039]] |
| **6.0** (2024) | immersion (metamarketing) | phygital natives (Gen Z and Alpha) | spatial computing, XR, generative AI | [[MKT-041-marketing-6-0-is-metamarketing-after-multichannel-and\|MKT-041]] |

Four threads run through every stage: **human-centricity** (5.0 presents itself as the synthesis of 3.0 and 4.0), **the shift of power to the customer** (horizontal marketing, communities, co-creation), **sustainability** (from the Millennium Development Goals in 3.0 to the SDGs in 5.0 and 6.0) and **the 5A path**, carried from 4.0 into the later books.

The stages coexist: the authors say many marketers still practice 1.0 or 2.0, and STP and the 4Ps remain universal. **Comparison with the textbooks.** The first two steps match Grewal and Levy's eras (production, then market) and the Kotler-Armstrong philosophies in the classic textbooks (MKT-003); the X.0 series adds new steps where the textbooks stop at "value" or at the societal concept. The narrative is rewritten from one book to the next (MKT-048).

## Application

- Client diagnosis: which "X.0" does the client actually work in (product, segments, values, digital, data, immersion)? The recommendation should climb at most one step at a time.
- Use the table as a reading map: each row points to its notes.
- In a pitch, do not sell "Marketing 6.0" to a client that does not yet have a working omnichannel (4.0).

## Example

Coca-Cola appears in every book, each time through the lens of the stage: in 3.0 it is a cultural brand ("I'd Like to Teach the World to Sing"), in 4.0 it is the example of a loyalty program (My Coke Rewards), and in 6.0 it is the pioneer of immersive experiences (Coca-Cola Creations, with AR and Fortnite).

## Questions

- How much of this evolution is historical description and how much is a consulting construct that justifies "the next book"?
- What other histories of marketing would contradict this periodization?
