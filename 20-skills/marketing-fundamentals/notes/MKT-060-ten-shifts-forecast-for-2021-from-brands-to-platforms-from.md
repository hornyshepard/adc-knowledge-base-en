# Ten shifts forecast for 2021: from brands to platforms, from mass media to memes and movements

> **The idea in one sentence:** in 2016, Wuebben set out ten "from… to…" shifts for marketing in 2021. Read from 2026, they show which directions were solid (platforms, memes, niches, AI) and which were tech enthusiasm (mass-market VR).

## Explanation

The ten shifts (ch. 10) and a retrospective check (the assessment is this note's, not the book's):

| # | From… to… | What Wuebben says | In 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | brands → **platforms** | value chains are subsumed by value networks; APIs, ecosystems (Facebook's three-stage game plan) | confirmed |
| 2 | mass media → **memes and movements** | Dawkins: a meme as a "package of culture"; *memejacking*; movements around a shared purpose | confirmed |
| 3 | brand messages → **multi-sensory experiences** | the ads that work are experiences on camera: TNT's "Push For Drama", the Febreze commercials with blindfolded people, Droga5's giant dominos for Prudential | partly |
| 4 | storytelling through **virtual reality** | VR as the standard by 2021 | not confirmed |
| 5 | pricing/benefits → **genuine passion** | the Millennials' "passion economy" | partly |
| 6 | planning around products → **adaptive strategy around customers** | a Bayesian approach, profiles updated continuously | partly |
| 7 | uninformed guessing → **data-driven simulations** | you test in simulated environments before launch | partly |
| 8 | Web 2.0 → **semantic web** | Knowledge Graph, Hummingbird, Schema.org | partly (AI search) |
| 9 | human thinking → **cognitive enhancement** | IBM Watson; an AI-edited David Attenborough video that audiences liked better | confirmed, later and differently |
| 10 | mass market → **niche explosion** | music: Kelly's "Four Stages of Flowing": fixed → free → flowing → opening | confirmed |

For the new strategy, Wuebben recommends three metrics (awareness, sales, advocacy) with a single primary objective. He also recommends innovation teams separate from strategy, and tolerance for failure (Michael Schrage's 5x5x5 Rapid Innovation Method) (ch. 10).

**What the source is worth.** Wuebben's book (relevance 3/5) is a secondhand synthesis, and many of its forecasts missed. The list stays useful precisely as a retrospective check: it shows which kind of claim about the future holds up (MKT-059).

## Application

- Use the list as a seminar exercise: each student picks a shift and looks for evidence for and against it.
- Build on the confirmed trends (platforms, memes, niches) and treat the rest as hypotheses.

## Example

The three "experiential" ads cited (TNT, Febreze, Droga5/Prudential) share one mechanism: real people in a real situation, on camera. That shows why the line between activation and TV advertising has blurred.

## Questions

- Why did the forecasts about people's behavior (memes, niches) mostly come true, and the ones about hardware (VR, holograms) less so?
- Which ten shifts would you write today for 2031?
