# For services, the mix becomes the 7Ps: people, process and physical evidence are added

> **The idea in one sentence:** when the product is an intangible experience, the four classic Ps are no longer enough: the people who deliver the service, the processes behind the scenes and the tangible indications of quality also become marketing decisions.

## Explanation

Policarpio (SRC-002) presents the extended mix as the **7Ps of marketing**: product, price, promotion, place, plus:

| Added P | What it means | Examples from the book |
|---|---|---|
| People | the employees who take and process inquiries, orders and complaints; the "face" of the organization | customer service by email, phone calls and live chat; they differentiate the offering if they are trained, empowered and motivated |
| Process | the flow that makes the customer journey smooth, efficient and customer-friendly | how long does a response take? what happens after an order is placed? |
| Physical evidence | the tangible indication of quality, useful to customers who pay before the service is provided | a hotel's clean, comfortable rooms; for an agency: the website, testimonials, case studies |

The author credits the classic marketing mix to James Culliton (1948), with the 4P formulation from Jerome McCarthy. The attribution is simplified: Culliton proposed the metaphor of the manager as a "mixer of ingredients", and the term "marketing mix" was popularized by Neil Borden.

**Convergences.** Grewal and Levy (SRC-001) reach the same idea by another road: services are intangible, inseparable, heterogeneous and perishable, so they need tangible cues and trained employees. In retailing, they add two Ps to the four: **presentation** (the store) and **personnel** (the *six Ps* of retailing). Kotler (Marketing 6.0) rebuilds the "third place" from exactly these elements, physical evidence, process and people, augmented digitally (EXP-022). Batat goes further and replaces each P with a decision about the experience (EXP-007).

## Application

- For any service, check that the people, the process and the evidence confirm the promise in the ad. Otherwise the communication gap appears.
- Physical evidence is creative territory: uniforms, spaces, packaging, the website and even the contract communicate the brand.
- When you present an agency at a pitch, apply the 7Ps to yourself: your portfolio and case studies are your physical evidence.

## Example

For an agency, the website with testimonials and case studies is the physical evidence of a service the client pays for before receiving it (SRC-002, p. 33).
