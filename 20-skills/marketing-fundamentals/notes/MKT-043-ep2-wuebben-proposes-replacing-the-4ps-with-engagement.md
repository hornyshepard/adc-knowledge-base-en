# EP²: Wuebben proposes replacing the 4Ps with Engagement, Experiences, Personalization, Passion

> **The idea in one sentence:** for Wuebben, the 4Ps are centered on the company; a mix for the prosumer age has to be built around the relationship: where you meet people, what experience they live, how personal it is and what passion you convey.

## Explanation

Wuebben criticizes the McCarthy/Kotler mix (product, price, promotion, place): all four elements are framed in the context of the company, which assumes it has the pole position in the relationship. He also reviews the alternatives: the classic 4Cs (consumer wants and needs, convenience, cost, communication; not to be confused with the 4Cs of Marketing 4.0), the 7Ps and John Jantsch's version (*Passion, Personality, Positioning, Purpose*). He rejects them all as not ideal for 2021–2030 and also proposes a new definition of marketing: genuinely connecting with people who are interested in what you do, so that together you can positively impact their lives.

| Component | What it means for Wuebben |
|---|---|
| **Engagement** | where and how you meet your audience: the platform; a two-way relationship, including user-generated ads |
| **Experiences** | we do not buy products but experiences, feelings, validation; the question is why we should sell |
| **Personalization** | personalized experiences everywhere (Amazon as the benchmark) |
| **Passion** | evangelism: a mission so inspiring that customers share it, through storytelling and narrative |

The common denominator: all four are about the **relationship** with people.

**Assessment.** Wuebben is the most radical critic of the 4Ps in the library. Kotler translates them into the 4Cs (MKT-035), Batat turns them into the 7Es, Sachdev keeps them and adds digital questions. EP², however, loses two decisions: price and distribution appear nowhere, even though they still have to be decided (MKT-020).

## Application

- Use EP² as an audit grid for a campaign: where is the engagement (two-way, or only a like button)? What experience stays in memory? What is personalized? What authentic passion shows?
- Do not drop the 4Ps as a list of decisions; use EP² to check whether the decisions serve the relationship.

## Example

Wuebben says Apple does not sell a phone but belonging to a type of person: "you are an iPhone type of person". This illustrates Experiences and Passion without any classic P.

## Questions

- Is EP² truly a *mix* (decision variables) or a philosophy of the relationship?
- Wuebben finds it crazy that business schools still teach the 4Ps. What would Grewal and Levy answer?
