# Marketing 6.0 is metamarketing: after multichannel and omnichannel, an experience in which customers perceive no distinction between physical and digital

> **The idea in one sentence:** Marketing 6.0 defines metamarketing as the approach that transcends the boundaries between the physical and digital worlds, providing an immersive experience in which customers perceive no distinction between the two.

## Explanation

The authors sum up the series: 3.0 brought sustainability, 4.0 content marketing and omnichannel, 5.0 artificial intelligence (with ChatGPT as the recent leap). The next step is driven by Generation Z and Generation Alpha, by short-form videos, by conversational commerce and by immersive technologies.

**The convergence of traditional and digital marketing** (Fig. 1.2) has three steps:

| Approach | The logic | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Multichannel** | multiple channels, each used independently; "traditional" and "digital" customers on separate journeys | TV for older audiences, Instagram for younger ones |
| **Omnichannel** | a single funnel, different roles for each channel, a consistent experience | *webrooming* (online at the top, store at the bottom) vs. *showrooming* |
| **Metamarketing** | a fully immersive customer journey: digital experiences in physical spaces or real-life experiences in virtual environments | AR, VR, metaverse |

The prefix "meta" means "beyond". E-commerce was only about 15 percent of total US retail sales in 2022, so the physical remains central, but it has to be augmented.

**Three layers** (Fig. 1.3): **enablers** (IoT captures the data, AI processes it, spatial computing shapes the experience, AR and VR are the interface, blockchain is the infrastructure), **environments** (extended realities and metaverses) and **experiences** (multisensory, spatial and metaverse marketing; the goal is an immersive and hybrid experience, EXP-024).

**How to read it from 2026.** The book appeared after the 2022 decline of the NFT and metaverse hype and itself acknowledges that most experiences will remain physical. The bet on the metaverse aged quickly; the solid part is the augmentation of physical space (MKT-059).

## Application

- Place the client on the multi–omni–meta scale: does it have separate channels, integrated channels or immersive experiences?
- Do not start with the metaverse. Immersion often begins with augmenting the store, the event or the packaging.
- Use the three layers as the structure of a proposal: which technology, in which environment, for which experience.

## Example

Coca-Cola Creations: Starlight, a flavor that envisions how outer space might taste, opens an augmented-reality concert with Ava Max when the can is scanned, and Zero Sugar Byte comes with a virtual island in Fortnite. The physical product becomes the gateway to a digital experience.

## Questions

- Is metamarketing a new stage or only omnichannel with more spectacular technology?
- What still holds from the bet on the metaverse?
