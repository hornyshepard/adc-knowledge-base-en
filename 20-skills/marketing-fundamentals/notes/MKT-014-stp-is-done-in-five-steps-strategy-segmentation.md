# STP is done in five steps: strategy, segmentation, attractiveness, choice of target, positioning

> **The idea in one sentence:** segmentation, targeting and positioning (STP) turn a vague market into a chosen group of people and a clear position in their minds, in five steps that can be repeated.

## Explanation

In the marketing plan, STP is step 3. Grewal and Levy break it into five steps (Exhibit 9.1):

| Stage | Step | The question |
|---|---|---|
| Segmentation | 1. Establish the overall strategy or objectives | What does the firm want, according to its mission and its SWOT? |
| Segmentation | 2. Use segmentation methods | By what criteria do I divide the market, and how do I describe each segment? |
| Targeting | 3. Evaluate segment attractiveness | Is the segment identifiable, substantial, reachable, responsive, profitable? |
| Targeting | 4. Select a target market | Undifferentiated, differentiated, concentrated or micromarketing? |
| Positioning | 5. Identify and develop a positioning strategy | How do we want to be understood, clearly and distinctively, relative to competitors? |

Two observations by the authors:

- **The process is not linear.** You can start with a strategy and change it as you learn how attractive the segments are.
- **Segmentation follows from the SWOT.** The Botticelli example (Italian sauces): strength, quality ingredients; weakness, nothing for people who avoid carbohydrates; opportunity, health-conscious and vegan consumers. The result: a vegan Bolognese sauce made with soy.

**Who contests STP.** In Marketing 3.0, Kotler and his co-authors proposed "communitization" in place of STP; in Marketing 4.0 they came back to it: STP remains valid as long as it is made transparent to customers, and communities "confirm" or "ignore" the brand (MKT-035). Segmentation with AI turns the segment into a temporary state, recalculated from behavior (AIM-017).

## Application

- Every good creative brief contains the results of STP: who the target is (steps 2 to 4) and what position must be strengthened (step 5). If they are missing, ask for them.
- Do not jump straight to "our target is women aged 25 to 45". That is a demographic description, not yet an evaluated target.
- Use STP for your own portfolio as well: which agencies you target and how you position yourself against other juniors.

## Example

Impossible Foods and Beyond Meat (ch. 9) do not target vegans, but meat eaters worried about health or the climate. The consequences show up in the whole mix: the product sits in the meat aisle, the packaging avoids vegetarian codes, and 93% of the shoppers who bought Beyond Burgers also put meat in the same basket.

## Questions

- When is it better to start from the desired positioning and look for the right segment?
- Does STP still make sense in the era of individual algorithmic targeting?
