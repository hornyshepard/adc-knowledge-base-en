# When the copy is free, value moves into what cannot be copied: Kevin Kelly's generatives

> **The idea in one sentence:** in the digital economy, copies cost almost nothing, so people pay for qualities generated at the time of the transaction, which cannot be cloned: personalization, interpretation, embodiment and discoverability.

## Explanation

Wuebben takes from Kevin Kelly's *The Inevitable* the idea of **generative** value: a quality that must be generated at the time of the exchange, for that particular transaction, in real time. It cannot be copied, stored or faked, which is why it is "better than free" (ch. 14). The four generatives described in the book:

| Generative | What you pay for | Example from the book |
|---|---|---|
| **Personalization** | adapting the free copy to you; it requires an ongoing conversation, so it creates stickiness | a book custom edited to reflect your previous reading background |
| **Interpretation** | the meaning and the instructions | sequencing your genome costs nothing, the "manual for your genes" is expensive |
| **Embodiment** | physical, live presence | the music is free, the live performance costs something |
| **Discoverability** | being found among millions of works | none |

Kelly names four more: immediacy, patronage, authenticity and accessibility. His conclusion, quoted by Wuebben: we use technology to produce commodities, and we make experiences so as not to become a commodity ourselves.

**The link with the textbooks** (the note's own reading): it is the same move that Grewal and Levy describe as differentiation through the outer layers of the product, services and experience, when the core product becomes a commodity (MKT-021). Kelly takes it to the limit: when the core product costs zero, only the outer layers remain.

## Application

- For a content brand (podcast, publication, musician), ask: if the core product is free, which generative are we selling? Early access, a personalized edition, a live event, curation?
- For the agency: core production is commoditized (AI, platform studios). Value remains in interpretation (strategy, insight) and in smart distribution.

## Example

Wuebben uses Spotify as a picture of the future: a huge catalog, available anytime, at little or no cost. What gets paid for is the experience, from the live concert to the personalization of playlists (ch. 8, 14).

## Questions

- Can authenticity be faked? What happens when AI perfectly imitates "embodiment"?
- Which generative does a luxury brand actually sell?
