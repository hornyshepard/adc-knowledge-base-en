# The communication target has layers (main target, core target and influence targets) and is reached through push or pull

> **The idea in one sentence:** the marketing target is the one you want to sell to; the communication target is the one you want to make the product known to, and it also includes the people who influence buyers.

## Explanation

*La boîte à outils de la publicité* separates two questions: who do we want to sell to (the marketing target, *cible marketing*) and who do we want to make the product known to (the communication target, *cible de communication*). The layers:

- **The main target** (*cible principale*): the people you must make aware of the product in order to make them buy. It is described qualitatively (obstacles, motivations) and quantitatively (number, location, age, socio-professional category, income). It can include customers to retain or to "wake up" and prospects to convert.
- **The core target** (*cœur de cible*): the subset of the main target with the highest potential; it concentrates the budget effort and the pressure of the message.
- **The secondary or influence target**: opinion leaders, journalists, sales advisers, the sales force, distributors, authorities. With a small budget, it can become the main target, used like a channel.

Two strategies for reaching the target:

| Strategy | Mechanism | Dominant objective | Tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pull** | draws the end consumer toward the brand | cognitive (awareness) | media and non-media advertising, social networks, the web |
| **Push** | pushes the product toward the target through intermediaries | conative (behavior) | the sales force, B2B, incentives, sales tools, attractive stores |

The ideal is to mix the two. Pull needs a larger budget, because it exerts stronger advertising pressure.

Percy and Rosenbaum-Elliott define the target differently: by behavior toward the brand, in five buyer groups, with demographics used only for the profile ([[ADV-015-the-target-audience-is-chosen-from-five-buyer-groups-and|ADV-015]]).

## Application

- Remember that a target is a person, not a structure. With an association: are you talking to its leaders or to its members? The message, the obstacles and the motivations differ.
- For each layer of the target, note a dedicated action and its budget. A target without an action is decoration.
- On a tight budget, ask whether prescribers (doctors, pharmacists, influencers, salespeople) are not the more efficient route.

## Example

An over-the-counter drug: the main target is adults with the symptom, the core target is mothers of young children, and the influence target is pharmacists, who recommend it at the counter (push).

## Questions

- Does digital hypertargeting still leave room for the notion of a "core target"?
- When does it become dangerous to rely only on the prescriber target? The authors say it is too risky and restrictive.
