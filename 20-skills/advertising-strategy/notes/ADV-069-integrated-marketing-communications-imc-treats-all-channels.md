# Integrated marketing communications (IMC) treats all channels as one whole: for clarity, consistency and maximum impact

> **The idea in one sentence:** IMC is promotion understood as a system: advertising, PR, sales promotion, personal selling, direct marketing and online, combined so that each one supports and extends the message of the others.

## Explanation

Grewal and Levy define **integrated marketing communications** (IMC) as the communication disciplines combined to provide clarity, consistency and maximum communicative impact. The difference from the old approach: the channels no longer each have their own control; they are parts of a whole. An IMC strategy has three elements: **the consumer** (how they receive and decode messages), **the channels** and the **evaluation** of the results. The authors place the channels on two axes (Exhibit 18.3):

| | Offline | Online |
|---|---|---|
| Passive | advertising, public relations, sales promotions (coupons), direct marketing (catalogs) | direct marketing (e-mail marketing) |
| Interactive | personal selling, sales promotions (contests), direct marketing (telemarketing) | mobile marketing, blogs, social media |

No channel is better in itself; the aim is for the sum to exceed the channels taken separately.

**The stricter definition.** Percy and Rosenbaum-Elliott start from objectives, not channels. A campaign is integrated only if it has a common set of communication objectives, a single positioning and a consistent look and feel across all media, with an execution coordinated by the manager. A direct mail piece unrelated to the advertising, or a sponsorship unrelated to the brand, is not part of the campaign. The two definitions converge on the practical rule: each channel has its own role and carries the same central message.

## Application

- Write the role of each channel in one sentence (why it is there, what it adds). A channel without its own role is just noise.
- Check consistency: the same central message and the same look and feel, adapted to the format, not different messages on each channel.
- Coordinate IMC with distribution and stock: a promotion with no product on the shelf destroys credibility.

## Example

BMW #roadtocoachella: a caravan of BMW i series hybrid and electric cars driven by big-name acts (Khalid in an i8 wrapped to reflect the cover art of his album), streamed live by influencers, with stops at dealers, festival parties and a photo booth. Every element tells the same story: the road.

## Questions

- IMC assumes a single "conductor". Who is it today: the client, the creative agency or the media agency?
- Can too much integration make a campaign monotonous on channels that need a different register (TikTok compared with TV)?
