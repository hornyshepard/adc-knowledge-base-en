# Congruent sponsorship is remembered better, but it can be misattributed to the category leader, and its effect on sales remains poorly proven

> **The idea in one sentence:** sponsorship links the brand to something already loved, but the audience reconstructs the sponsors from fit and from the size of the brands, so a congruent but smaller sponsor risks "donating" its investment to the big competitor.

## Explanation

Sponsorship increases familiarity, transfers affect through evaluative conditioning and creates balance (Heider). Fennis and Stroebe note that a 10-second television sponsorship message was as effective as a 30-second spot (Olson & Thjømøe, 2009). The problem is memory: viewers pay little attention to sponsor billboards and guess the sponsor using two heuristics, **relatedness or congruity** (a track and field event "should" have an athletic shoe sponsor) and **market prominence** (the well-known brand is guessed more often). At the 2014 Football World Cup, 30% thought the sponsor was Nike, although it was Adidas, helped by a Nike ambush marketing campaign. Their solution: in congruent sponsorships, emphasize the **distinctive attributes** of the brand (the Adidas three stripes), not the fit.

Percy and Rosenbaum-Elliott separate **sponsorship** (an ongoing relationship, for example with an athlete) from **event marketing** (a single event, for example the Olympic Games). They ask three questions before signing: will it communicate the brand's positioning? does it offer good media value, that is, target audience reach, in what reach pattern and at what cost? can you measure if it has worked? The fit has to link the event to the brand's **benefit**: Oktoberfest suits a beer or a sausage brand, not tea or biscuits; Hellmann's Extra Light mayonnaise suits an organization involved in heart research, Häagen-Dazs does not. Their conclusion is cautious: sponsorship can support brand awareness and brand attitude, but there is very little evidence that it adds directly to the bottom line, and at the Olympics the lift for sponsors is small and short-lived.

## Application

- Answer Percy's three questions before the contract, and write down the benefit the event dramatizes.
- In a congruent sponsorship, invest in activating the brand's distinctive codes, not in the message "we are a good fit".
- Prepare the defense against ambush: your own activations, clear rights, communication before and after the event.

## Example

The 2014 World Cup: Adidas the official sponsor, Nike the "winner" in the memory of 30% of respondents.
