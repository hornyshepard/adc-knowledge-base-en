# Any communication can produce four effects, but brand awareness and brand attitude are always objectives

> **The idea in one sentence:** communication objectives are not sales but mental effects tied to the brand, chosen from a small set; Percy and the French school name the set differently but converge, and Percy adds that brand awareness and brand attitude are never missing.

## Explanation

Percy and Rosenbaum-Elliott take four **communication effects** from Rossiter and Percy. They mirror the questions any buyer asks: do I need it? which brands do I know? what do I think of them? will I buy one?

| Effect (Percy) | What it means | When it becomes an objective | French family |
|---|---|---|---|
| Category need | wanting the product to satisfy a need | only when it must be reminded or "sold" | cognitive (*faire savoir*) |
| Brand awareness | being able to identify the brand well enough to buy it | **always** | cognitive |
| Brand attitude | evaluating the brand as a means of satisfying the motivation | **always** | affective (*faire aimer*), including removing the obstacles (*lever les freins*) |
| Brand purchase intention | "I think I'd like to try that" | rarely for advertising, always for promotion | conative (*faire agir*): trial, visit, traffic |

**Where they converge.** *La boîte à outils de la publicité*, like Percy, separates marketing objectives (quantitative: sales, share) from communication objectives (effects in the target's mind). Both require that an effect become an objective only if it is not already present. Percy even observes that most models of involvement include the same triad, cognition, affect and conation, which in marketing means brand awareness, brand attitude and trial.

**Where they differ.** Barre and Gayrard-Carrera link each family to a single problem (awareness, image or behavior) and ask for **priorities**, because priorities steer the media plan. Percy does not leave attitude out: even an awareness campaign must build attitude. *La bible de la communication* adds that each objective should be SMART, with an owner and KPIs.

Reading note: Percy's 6th edition works with four effects; the fifth effect of the older works, purchase facilitation, appears only as a bibliographic reference.

## Application

- In the brief, write which effects are objectives and why. If attitude is missing, the brief is wrong.
- Write the priority objective so that it can be measured: "aided awareness from 20% to 35% among 25–34-year-olds by December".
- Do not put "sales" as a communication objective: the sale is the target audience's action, and the mental effect makes it possible.

## Example

At the launch of freeze-dried Nescafé, the likely objectives were category need (interest in soluble coffee), brand awareness, brand attitude (better taste thanks to freeze-drying) and trial intention, supported by a promotion.

## Questions

- For a new brand in a new category, how many objectives do you have at once? Do they fit in a single execution?
- Is social media "engagement" an affective or a conative objective?
