# There is a single communication problem, of awareness, image or behavior, never all three at once

> **The idea in one sentence:** like a doctor who makes a diagnosis from the symptoms, the agency reduces the SWOT to a few lines of diagnosis and to a single communication problem, framed as a question, which will condition all the objectives.

## Explanation

In *La boîte à outils de la publicité*, analysis and strategy are linked by two tools.

**1. The SWOT for communication.** Strengths and weaknesses are what the firm can control: the brand's history and awareness, its image, the elements of the mix, consumers (expectations, obstacles (*freins*), motivations), sales and market share. Opportunities and threats are what it cannot control: the market, competitors, potential consumers, distribution. The authors' rules: one slide, no attempt at exhaustiveness, factual, ranked, with 3–4 main guidelines. It is not a formal exercise to justify ready-made solutions.

**2. The diagnosis and the problem.** The steps:

1. write the diagnosis (*diagnostic*) in a few lines, using only the key points of the SWOT;
2. identify the main directions;
3. frame the issue (*problématique*) as a question;
4. choose the major problem: **awareness, image or behavior**, never all three. Two can be logically linked: a little-known brand is hard to position against its competitors, so an awareness problem brings an image problem with it;
5. bring the brief's indications into the issue;
6. check coherence: no element that does not appear in the diagnosis can be put into the problem.

The diagnosis also defines the main axis of the strategy: something to **modify**, to **enhance** (*valoriser*) or to **create**.

## Application

- Write the problem as a question of the type: "How do we get [target] to [change] toward [brand], when [obstacle]?"
- If you have three problems, rank them and choose the one that, once solved, unblocks the others.
- Return to the problem at each stage of the recommendation: every solution must answer it.

## Example

An organic cosmetics brand, liked by those who know it but rarely bought: the SWOT shows a good image, weak distribution and low awareness. The problem is not one of image; it is one of awareness, possibly of behavior (trial).

## Questions

- Is the "single problem" rule realistic for a client paying for a large campaign?
- How does the communication problem differ from the marketing problem (price, distribution, product)?
