# Public relations gains credibility precisely because the space is not bought, but it loses control of the message and the link with the brand

> **The idea in one sentence:** PR builds the image and handles negative stories, and people believe it more than advertising; the price of that credibility is weak control over what appears and the risk that the audience does not link the message to the brand.

## Explanation

Grewal and Levy define **public relations** as the organizational function that manages the firm's communications to build and maintain a positive image, handle or head off unfavorable stories, and maintain positive relationships with the media. Its importance has grown because the other forms of communication have become more expensive, consumers are skeptical, and the coverage obtained seems more objective, since the firm does not buy the space. Three tools: placement at media events (designers on the red carpet), cause-related marketing (Chili's and St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, more than $71 million raised) and event sponsorship (the Red Bull Air Race).

Percy and Rosenbaum-Elliott confirm the advantages, but add three corrections:

- **PR is not free.** It has been estimated that some 10 billion US dollars is spent annually on PR worldwide.
- **It has two strategies**: *proactive* (planned activity that attracts attention and builds brand attitude) and *reactive* (damage control, for example after a product recall).
- **It has a major disadvantage**: it is almost impossible to ensure that the message will be exposed or presented the way the brand hoped. And because it is not seen as advertising, it is much harder for the target audience to link the message to the brand.

They use Harris's term, **marketing public relations** (MPR): PR in support of marketing objectives, about 70% of PR activities in the mid-1990s. With social media, MPR has to be brought in early in planning, or else the campaign lacks *talk value*, a story that the media and people can pick up.

## Application

- Think from the start about which part of the idea can become news or conversation. An idea that generates free coverage multiplies its budget.
- Build the link with the brand into the PR story itself; do not rely on the audience making it on its own.
- In cause marketing, the link between brand and cause must be credible and lasting, or it looks like opportunism.

## Example

Miller Lite's PR and advertising agencies jointly created the CanTroller, a video game controller integrated with a full can of beer. An extensive video and a few simple visuals were picked up by a variety of media, then talked about on social media.

## Questions

- When does "orchestrated" PR become a form of manipulating the press?
- Is paid influencer marketing still PR (earned) or has it become advertising (paid)?
