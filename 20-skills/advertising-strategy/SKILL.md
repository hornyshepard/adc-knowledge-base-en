# Advertising and communication strategy (ADV)

The skill has one backbone: the planning process of Percy and Rosenbaum-Elliott (*Strategic Advertising Management*), in five steps, preceded by a diagnosis and followed by the campaign. The other sources (Fernández Gómez, *La boîte à outils de la publicité*, Milavsky, Fennis & Stroebe, Grewal & Levy, Kellogg on Branding) are attached to the step they help with, and the notes say explicitly where the authors converge and where they contradict each other.

## What mastering this skill means

Observable competencies ("you can…"):

- **C1** You can explain the difference between advertising and promotion and between marketing objectives and communication objectives, then choose the right communication effects: category need, brand awareness, brand attitude, brand purchase intention.
- **C2** You can go through the five steps of planning (target audience, decision, positioning, communication strategy, media), starting from a single communication problem, and justify each link by the one before it.
- **C3** You can define the target audience by its behavior toward the brand (buyer groups, loyalty) and write a positioning that answers "what is it?" and "what does it offer?".
- **C4** You can place a brand, for a specific target audience, in a quadrant of the Rossiter-Percy grid and derive from it the creative tactics, the source, whether the ad must be liked, and the media requirements.
- **C5** You can compare the classic strategic mechanisms (USP, copy strategy, brand image, psychological axis, positioning, disruption) and say when each one fits.
- **C6** You can build a media plan and a budget from objectives: media chosen by processing requirements, minimum effective frequency, reach pattern, the task method.
- **C7** You can integrate advertising with promotion, PR, sponsorship, content and packaging in a campaign with the same objectives and the same look and feel.
- **C8** You can design the pretest and the tracking of a campaign and diagnose which step of the response sequence failed.
