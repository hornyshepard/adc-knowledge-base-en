# The agency of the future: six forces that are changing its business model

> **The idea in one sentence:** the classic agency is under pressure from six directions: technology that removes intermediaries, the platforms' creative studios, clients who do the work themselves, the competition for talent, ideas that come from outside the industry and audiences that have become media channels.

## Explanation

Wuebben describes six developments (ch. 11):

1. **Technology will continue to disintermediate.** The future model is a hybrid: a smaller core of senior staff sitting on top of a network of startups, technology companies and individuals.
2. **More competition from media companies.** Facebook's Creative Shop, Google's Creative Lab, YouTube Studio, but also The New York Times or BuzzFeed give away creativity to sell media space. The price of creative work becomes commoditized.
3. **Brands will do some of the work agencies do now.** Red Bull, Patagonia and GoPro produce their own content. Programmatic marketing and first-party data favor bringing activities in-house (*in-housing*).
4. **Co-opetition.** Bill Joy: "most of the smartest people work for someone else." The agency has to move from a closed system that sells solutions to an open system that helps the client ask the right questions and finds the right talent.
5. **Ideas come from everywhere.** Whit Hiler, an amateur turned down by agencies, won an open call from Victors & Spoils. His idea for Harley-Davidson ("No Cages") became the brand anthem.
6. **People are the new media channels.** Instead of ads placed in media, you create shareable and scalable ideas, and people spread them. The book cites Ipsos: content created by consumers is said to be 35 percent more memorable and 50 percent more trusted.

For Wuebben, every type of agency (creative, media, brand, SEO, social) becomes a "consumer (or prosumer) experience" agency, led by content strategists who architect the prosumer journey.

## Application

- For your career: your value does not lie in the execution that a platform studio can also do, but in questions, strategy, the idea and orchestrating a network.
- For positioning a small agency: an open network of specialists, not a closed portfolio of services.
- Build user-generated content into the idea, not as an add-on.

## Example

Seen from 2026, the Harley-Davidson "No Cages" case anticipated creative crowdsourcing platforms and, later, the creator economy, which moved part of the production of ideas outside agencies.

## Questions

- Which of the six forces has generative AI accelerated the most?
- If ideas can come from anywhere, what still justifies an agency's fee?
