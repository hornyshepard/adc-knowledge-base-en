# Advertising history and culture (HIS)

## What mastering this skill means

Observable competencies ("you can…"):

- **C1** You can explain why advertising and the brand are much older than printing and which functions they have kept (origin, quality, promise).
- **C2** You can describe how the advertising-funded press, the agency, its remuneration and the awards were built, and how each new medium repeated the same cycle.
- **C3** You can place a creative style or method in its genealogy (reason-why, image, USP, Big Idea, planning) and recognize an old idea under a new name.
- **C4** You can use classic campaigns as verified arguments: invented occasions, launches, brand characters, demonstrations.
- **C5** You can explain how advertising techniques passed into wartime propaganda and political advertising.
- **C6** You can judge a campaign ethically and legally, with precedents: deception and proof, self-regulation, disguised advertising, subliminal, shock, tobacco, representation.
- **C7** You can check historical "facts" before using them in a pitch and flag where the sources disagree.
