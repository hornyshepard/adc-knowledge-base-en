# Flow: total absorption appears when the challenge is high and matched to the person's skills

> **The idea in one sentence:** immersion is the way into an experience; flow (Csikszentmihalyi) is its highest degree of intensity, in which people forget time and themselves because the activity demands exactly as much as they can handle.

## Explanation

Batat links flow to Maslow's **peak experience**: instants of maximum contentment and fulfillment. Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi describes flow as an optimal psychological state in which the individual is completely absorbed. Concentration leaves no room for worries, self-consciousness disappears and the sense of time is distorted. The activity is **autotelic**: it is done for its own sake, even when it is hard.

Conditions: skills must match the challenge, and the challenge must be high. Doing the laundry rarely creates flow; writing a novel, painting or dancing does, as long as it suits the person's abilities. Studies with the *Experience Sampling Method* show, for example, that American teens experience flow in 13% of the time spent watching television, 34% in their hobbies and 44% in sports and games. Flow appears only during the activity and comes in episodes; a long experience is a succession of immersions.

Batat gives **five rules** for designing experiences that favor flow:
1. suit the experience to the consumer's skills;
2. make it meaningful;
3. help consumers set up their own goals;
4. give them feedback;
5. let them control the environmental elements that allow immersion.

Flow also has a negative side: it is associated with addictions (video games) and risky behaviors.

**Online flow** (Hoffman & Novak, 1996) is a pleasant state during navigation, facilitated by interactivity and accompanied by a loss of self-awareness.

## Application

- For an app, a branded game or an activation: define difficulty levels, clear goals and immediate feedback.
- Do not overload the experience with stimuli that interrupt absorption (pop-ups, data requests).
- Take the ethical risk into account: a mechanism that is too sticky can become a reputation problem.

## Example

The Renault New Deal website (2008) aimed at flow through three types of factors: social (the virtual agents Nicolas and Isabelle, who answered questions), design (a control bar, interactive video) and ambiance (a 3D decor that looked like the Renault store on the Champs-Élysées).

## Questions

- Can an ordinary retail experience produce flow, or only immersion?
- Where is the line between an engaging experience and one that creates addiction?
