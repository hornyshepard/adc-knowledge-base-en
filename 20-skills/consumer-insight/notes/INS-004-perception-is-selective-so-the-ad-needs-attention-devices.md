# Perception is selective, so the ad needs attention devices: emotion, personification, staging, surprise, humor

> **The idea in one sentence:** consumers filter their environment heavily, so a message first has to be noticed, then decoded the way the sender meant it; attention is won with a few families of devices, but the device has to be congruent with the product.

## Explanation

*La boîte à outils de la publicité* describes perception as the process by which an individual chooses, organizes and interprets information to build a coherent picture of the world. In advertising the phases are: exposure → attention → decoding → response (*l'exposition à l'information, l'attention, le décodage et la réponse*). There are three filters:

- **selective attention** (*attention sélective*): you retain what concerns your needs ("j'ai besoin de changer ma machine à laver, je repère les promotions…" [I need to change my washing machine, so I spot the promotions…]), what you expect to see, and stimuli more intense than usual (70% off sales);
- **selective distortion** (*distorsion sélective*): you bend information to fit your expectations (the same product seems better under a brand you love);
- **selective retention** (*rétention sélective*): you forget a great deal and more easily retain the positive points of your favorite brand.

Hence, the authors say, the usefulness of repetition. The **five attention devices** (*procédés d'attention*):

| Device | Examples |
|---|---|
| Appeal to emotions (*appel aux émotions*) | beauty, love, self-sacrifice for a cause, sensuality, fear |
| Personification (*personnalisation*) | a mascot that embodies the brand's values and makes it easier to recognize |
| Staging (*mise en scène*, spectacle) | very short sketches, mini-comedies, animation |
| Surprise | the meeting of two seemingly foreign worlds, irony, suspense, hyperbole pushed to the point of myth |
| Humor | incongruous juxtaposition, diversion (*détournement*), exaggeration, playing against type (*contre-emploi*) |

Warnings: low involvement, advertising pressure and media saturation lead to indifference, weariness or hostility. A device with little congruence with the product attracts attention but can provoke rejection. A message that goes against the target's value system produces dissonance, which has to be reduced through pretests.

## Application

- Start from the references and values you share with the target: a message the target expects or wants gets through the filters more easily.
- Choose the attention device by the product, not by fashion: a bank's humor and a snack brand's humor have different limits.
- Check whether the device leads the eye to the brand or steals it from the brand.

## Example

The Oasis fruit characters (Ramon Tafraise, Mangue Debol) combine personification with wordplay: the mascot makes the brand recognizable, and the humor makes fans look forward to the episodes.

## Questions

- When is attention won "too well", so that people remember the joke but not the brand?
- On TikTok, attention is won or lost in the first two seconds. Which of the five devices work there?
