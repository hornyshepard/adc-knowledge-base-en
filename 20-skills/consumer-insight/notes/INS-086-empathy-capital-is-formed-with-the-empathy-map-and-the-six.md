# Empathy capital is formed with the empathy map and the six aspects of McLaren's model

> **The idea in one sentence:** empathy for the customer is not only a talent; it can be trained with a team tool (the empathy map) and with a ladder of skills that build upon each other (McLaren).

## Explanation

Batat argues that empathic employees, from engineers to salespeople, decode the hidden needs and emotions of customers and design experiences that go beyond the three usual dimensions of an offering: attractiveness, viability, sustainability. Professionalized empathy has to be customer-centric, focused on customer well-being, helpful, intentional, self-conscious and maintainable.

**1. The empathy map**, a collaborative tool developed by David Gray (XPLANE). The customer is drawn in the center, surrounded by questions:
- What are they thinking and feeling? What are their doubts and ambitions?
- What do their friends, associates and superiors say while they consume the product?
- What do they hear, perceive and see in that setting?
- What do they say and do? How does that change in public and in private?
- What bothers or worries them? What improvements do they need?

**2. McLaren's empathy model** (2013, from the area of health): six aspects that build upon each other. The first tends to occur automatically; the others can be learned:

| Aspect | In short |
|---|---|
| Emotion contagion | you feel that an emotion is happening or expected |
| Empathic accuracy | you identify emotions and intentions correctly, in yourself and in the customer |
| Emotion regulation | you manage your own emotions so you are not overwhelmed |
| Perspective-taking | you see the situation through the customer's eyes |
| Concern for others | you show compassion and real concern |
| Perspective engagement | you act skillfully on everything you have perceived |

## Application

- In strategy workshops, fill in an empathy map for each segment before you write the brief; use quotes from research, not assumptions.
- Use the McLaren ladder as a training plan for event or store staff.

## Example

IKEA's "Retail Therapy" (2016) renamed products on its website after common Google searches: the sofa became "My partner snores", the microwave oven "My mother cannot cook". Batat cites it as an example of empathy expressed in communication. Always #LikeAGirl (P&G) is the chapter's other example: the brand takes the perspective of girls who lose confidence at puberty.

## Questions

- An empathy map built only from the team's assumptions can reinforce stereotypes instead of correcting them. How do you prevent that?
- Can empathy be trained, or can it only be selected for when hiring?
