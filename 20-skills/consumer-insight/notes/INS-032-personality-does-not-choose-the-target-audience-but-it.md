# Personality does not choose the target audience, but it changes how the message is processed, through stable traits and temporary states

> **The idea in one sentence:** you do not select the audience by personality, but you need to know how self-esteem, intelligence, extroversion or anxiety change attention, comprehension and acceptance, especially for the copy of an ad.

## Explanation

The authors distinguish personality **traits** (enduring) from personality **states** (temporary activations that operate like a trait). Both matter for processing, especially for the verbal content of communication.

| Factor | Effect on processing |
|---|---|
| Self-esteem | With simple messages, people with high self-esteem are harder to persuade. With complex or specific messages, they are persuaded more easily. |
| Intelligence | More intelligent people deal better with complex messages and need fewer repetitions for high-involvement products. People whose first language is not English "should be thought of as less intelligent" as far as processing is concerned: English-language advertising in non-English-speaking markets needs simple copy. |
| Introversion / extroversion | One of the few traits correlated with behavior. Informational appeals (negative motives) work better with introverts. Positive appeals, such as social approval, work better with extroverts. |
| Anxiety state | Too much anxiety makes people tune the message out, physically or mentally (very gruesome social marketing campaigns, tire or insurance ads showing bad accidents). Moderate anxiety, if the person still pays attention, increases persuasion. |

States can be aroused by the context (a heartwarming program before the break), by the message itself (a very warm commercial) or by an **innate releasing mechanism**, such as the reaction to a baby or a puppy. A trait such as nurturance (a desire to look after and care for people) increases attention to a message about taking better care of your family.

## Application

- If you suspect a trait could influence the message, test it on a sample of the target buyer group.
- Calibrate the dose of fear in social communication. The pretest must check that attention does not break.
- For regional campaigns in English, simplify the syntax and put the idea in the image.

## Example

A road safety campaign with very graphic images may be avoided by exactly the drivers it targets. One that creates concern but immediately offers a simple action ("put your phone in the glove box") holds attention.

## Questions

- Why does research on traits often reach contradictory conclusions, as the authors observe?
- What does the media context mean for personality states on platforms with infinite scrolling?
