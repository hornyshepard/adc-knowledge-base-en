# Holbrook's value typology: eight values for analyzing any experience, physical or digital

> **The idea in one sentence:** Holbrook breaks the value customers look for into eight types, from efficiency to spirituality, and Batat uses them to compare in-store experiences with online ones.

## Explanation

The eight values, as Batat presents them:

| Value | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Efficiency** | the ratio of output to effort, often per unit of time |
| **Excellence** | the quality of the product or experience, its ability to achieve its purpose |
| **Status** | how consumption affects my image in the eyes of others, within a sociocultural context |
| **Esteem** | the reflective evaluation of one's own consumption and its effect on one's public image |
| **Play** | having fun within the consumption |
| **Aesthetics** | the beauty experienced in the experience |
| **Ethics** | how my consumption affects others and how they react to it |
| **Spirituality** | losing one's self, adoration, mystic experience |

For value-in-experience, Holbrook (1994) proposes three dimensions: **extrinsic / intrinsic** (a means to an end or an end in itself), **self-oriented / other-oriented** and **active / reactive** (I use the object directly, or I only appreciate it and respond to it).

**Batat's application to digital.** The studies she cites show that digital technologies enhance efficiency (convenience) and excellence (better decisions, price comparison sites), and favor play (flow when browsing, video games), aesthetics (images and stories that transport people into imaginary worlds) and linking value: online communities create feelings of "we-ness", and give status and approval. The conclusion: digital experiences appear **quantitatively** different from physical ones (more or less value), not **qualitatively**. Research on what makes them qualitatively different (transient, unstable, fragmented, polyvocal) is only beginning.

## Application

- Draw a value map for a brand experience: which values does it deliver today, which ones does the communication promise, which ones could it add?
- Use the table as an idea generator: what would the same category look like if it delivered play or ethics instead of efficiency?

## Example

A banking app delivers mainly efficiency and excellence; a runners' community (Nike+ in the book) adds play, status and linking value.

## Questions

- Do the values exclude or combine with each other? What happens when status conflicts with ethics?
- Do you agree that digital differs from physical only quantitatively? What example would contradict the claim?
