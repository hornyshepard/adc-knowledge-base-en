# Every advertising message ranks the six functions of language (Jakobson), and one of them must dominate

> **The idea in one sentence:** a message always has a sender, a receiver, a message, a channel, a code and a context; each of them corresponds to a function of language, and the analysis of an ad starts with the question of which function is primary and which are secondary.

## Explanation

*La boîte à outils de la publicité* uses Roman Jakobson's model (1963), still operational in the digital age:

| Factor | Function | In advertising |
|---|---|---|
| Sender (*émetteur*) | **expressive (emotive)** (*émotive*) | the brand puts itself forward and justifies its discourse through its qualities; this is the role of the proof in the copy strategy (for example, "German quality") |
| Receiver (*récepteur*) | **conative (impressive)** | the consumer is put on stage, often in the second person, with an injunction (*injonction*) to happiness, relaxation, balance, which flatters them |
| Context | **referential** (*référentielle*) | informative, denotative: it describes the product's features; needed for technical products |
| Contact (channel) | **phatic** (*phatique*) | everything that draws and holds attention: the gaze, the music, an onomatopoeia |
| Message | **poetic** (*poétique*) | play with the form of the message: rhyme, rhythm, the sublimation of the brand's world, especially in luxury |
| Code | **metalinguistic** (*métalinguistique*) | the message that talks about its own code, about language |

A few principles: the functions do not exclude each other, they coexist; the variety of messages comes from the way they rank them. Communication works only if the sender and the receiver share a **common code** (*code commun*), that is, a repertoire of prefabricated representations. The closer the sender's intention and the receiver's interpretation, the better the exchange. **Noise** (*bruit*) is everything that disturbs the transmission. The broad cultural context and the finer one (status, role, identity, the culture of the organization) determine how the message is read.

*La bible de la communication* puts the same idea on three levels: what I think (the intention), what I send (the message), what the other perceives (the effect). Effectiveness is « la concordance entre l'effet produit et l'intention initiale » [the agreement between the effect produced and the initial intention], and each person's filters (*filtres*: education, prejudice, experience) can spoil it. The same book holds that it is impossible not to communicate, and that the way you say something (the paraverbal and the nonverbal) defines the relationship more than the content.

## Application

- When you analyze an ad in a seminar, name the dominant function and two secondary ones. Then ask whether the choice serves the objective.
- When you write, decide in advance: am I talking about the brand (expressive), about you (conative), about the product (referential), or am I playing with the form (poetic)?
- Check the code: does the target understand the reference, the slang, the cultural allusion?

## Example

A luxury perfume commercial is dominated by the poetic function (the sublimated world) and the phatic one (the music and the gaze that hook you), with almost nothing referential. An ad for a laptop for professionals reverses the ranking: the referential dominates.

## Questions

- Which function dominates most of the ads in your feed? And what does that say about the category?
- Is the metalinguistic function (the ad about advertising) a sign that the genre is tired, or of complicity with the audience?
