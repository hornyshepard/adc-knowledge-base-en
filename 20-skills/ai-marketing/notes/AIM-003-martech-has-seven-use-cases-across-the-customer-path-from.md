# Martech has seven use cases across the customer path: from advertising and content to service CRM

> **The idea in one sentence:** marketing technology is not a single tool but seven distinct uses along the customer journey, and the challenge is to choose the ones that fit the strategy and integrate them into a frictionless experience.

## Explanation

Marketing 5.0 closes the chapter on the new CX with a checklist (Fig. 7.4, "Marketing Technology Use Cases in the New CX") that links the next tech to the touchpoints:

| # | Touchpoint | What the technology does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Advertising** | audience targeting and personas; **dynamic creative** (many combinations of copy and visuals produced quickly by AI); contextual advertising (the car ad appears while you read car reviews); programmatic media buying |
| 2 | **Content marketing** | analytics for the audience's needs, automated generation and curation of content, **dynamic content** on the website, based on behavior (Amazon, Netflix) |
| 3 | **Direct marketing** | personalized messages (so they are not perceived as spam), **product recommendation systems**, automated workflows, forecasting of conversion |
| 4 | **Sales CRM** | chatbots that capture and qualify leads (the top and middle of the funnel), automated account management, predictive sales forecasts |
| 5 | **Distribution channel** | contactless interfaces, robots, drones (JD.com during the pandemic), biometric payment, smart stores with IoT, AR and VR for a virtual "visit" (Prada) |
| 6 | **Product and service** | mass customization (Gillette, Levi's, Mercedes-Benz), dynamic pricing, "everything-as-a-service", predictive analytics for product development (PepsiCo and Black Swan) |
| 7 | **Service CRM** | a 24/7 chatbot that transfers the ticket to a human agent, the history of past interactions for agents, **churn detection** from social listening |

The authors' conclusion: with technology, marketers will leave the science part of marketing to machines and focus on the art.

## Application

- Use the table as a quick audit: for each of the seven points, note what the client already uses, what is missing and what would help most at the 5A stage where the path gets stuck.
- In performance pitches, propose dynamic creative with a system of variations (a core message plus modules), not dozens of independent executions.
- Do not implement everything: pick one or two use cases tied directly to the business objective.

## Example

HubSpot uses a chatbot to capture and nurture leads, a sales force for consultative selling to qualified leads, a high-touch team for onboarding, and the chatbot again for simple queries after the sale.

## Questions

- Dynamic creative optimizes the message by segment. What happens to brand consistency?
- Which use cases on the list have since become market standard and no longer differentiate?
