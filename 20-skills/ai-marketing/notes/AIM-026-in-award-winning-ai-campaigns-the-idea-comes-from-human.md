# In award-winning AI campaigns, the idea comes from human insight and the AI gives it scale: Cadbury, Nike "Never Done Evolving", Jardim Sonoro

> **The idea in one sentence:** in the few AI campaigns documented in the library, the concept was formulated by people, and the technology did what people could not do at that scale: thousands of versions, thousands of simulated games, dozens of visual directions.

## Explanation

The AI books in the library give almost no real creative cases. The cases come from two general sources, *Marketing 6.0* and *Shareworthy*:

| Campaign | The (human) idea | What the AI did | Reported result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cadbury #NotJustACadburyAd** (India, pandemic) | the brand lends its ambassador to local small businesses hit by the pandemic | recreated the face and voice of Shah Rukh Khan, so the actor seems to mention the names of local businesses; small companies produced their own versions of the ad | over 130,000 ads, 94 million views |
| **Nike / AKQA "Never Done Evolving"** (Nike's 50th anniversary) | 1999 Serena Williams plays 2017 Serena Williams | machine learning modeled each era's playing style from archive footage (decision making, shot selection, reaction time) and simulated 130,000 games | 1.7 million viewers of the grand final on YouTube; organic views 1,082% above other Nike content |
| **Jardim Sonoro "Blended with Nature"** (Dentsu Creative Portugal) | the festival moves to a bigger park; the music merges with nature | Midjourney generated the posters from the performers' names and words from nature; the team refined the output | signature posters; an "A.I. prompt master" appears in the credits |

The pattern is the same. Diego Machado, chief creative officer at AKQA São Paulo, recounts that the team first landed on the idea (Serena's evolution) and only then brought in the tech team. Kotler's Marketing 6.0 describes generative AI as a tool for personalized marketing at a micro level, and Cadbury is its example. Ivo Purvis (Jardim Sonoro) says that AI will force us to expand our creative frontiers, but that in the end it is all about the talent to master the tool. Guan Hin Tay (BBDO Singapore) compares AI to a knife: its value depends on the intention and the ethical code of the person using it.

## Application

- Write the idea in one sentence without the word "AI". If it does not hold up, you have a tech demo, not a campaign.
- Name exactly what the AI scales: versions, simulations, visual exploration. The rest (insight, concept, selection) stays with the team.
- When you use the face or voice of a real person, settle the consent and the label before production.

## Example

The useful counter-example is "Sick Beats" (Area 23): the idea of a vest that clears the airways of children with cystic fibrosis through music started from conversations with the families. The AI had a modest role: an AI-enabled sniffer searched 30 million songs in the Spotify library for the therapeutic 40Hz frequency.

## Questions

- All the cases come from award entries. What do we not see of the AI campaigns that did not work?
- What consent and what disclosure would you ask for today for an ad in which a celebrity "says" generated things?
