# In the new CX, machines are "cool" but humans are "warm": AI takes data and convergent thinking, humans keep insight, wisdom and empathy

> **The idea in one sentence:** the best customer experience does not come from replacing people with machines but from a division of labor in which the computer does what is logical and repetitive, and the human what is contextual, intuitive and empathetic.

## Explanation

The chapter opens with the Henn-na Hotel in Japan, the world's first robot-staffed hotel. The robots caused so many problems (one in-room robot mistook snoring for an inquiry and kept waking guests up) that the hotel "fired" half of them. Kotler, Kartajaya and Setiawan give three arguments for the human-machine symbiosis:

1. **Moravec's paradox**: it is relatively easy to make computers perform well on intelligence tests, but almost impossible to give them the perception and mobility skills of a one-year-old child. Conscious reasoning is easy to teach to machines; empathy and common sense are not.
2. **The knowledge management hierarchy** (Fig. 7.2, built on the DIKW model): *data, information and knowledge* belong to machines; *noise* (outliers, which only human judgment can tell apart from errors), *insight* (often found in anomalies and among extreme users) and *wisdom* belong to humans. The David Dao case (United, 2017) shows what happens when nobody overrides the algorithm ([[AIM-032-bias-in-ai-marketing-has-two-sources-the-data-the-model|AIM-032]]).
3. **Convergent vs. divergent thinking**: computers identify patterns; humans generate new ideas. In advertising, AI correlates color scheme, copy and layout with outcomes; Chase, using Persado, got copy with higher click-through rates than its human copywriters. Still, the authors say, no machine writes an agency brief, crafts a brand positioning that resonates or designs a novel campaign: AI only optimizes.

In the customer interface, automation serves speed (reservations, payments, predictable queries) and humans serve flexibility (consultation, hospitality, the unexpected). The risk is insensitivity: Microsoft's chatbot Tay was retired after only 16 hours.

## Application

- In an agency workflow, leave data and variations to AI (copy versions, creative testing) and keep the brief, the insight and the idea for people.
- In research, do not remove outliers automatically: they can be sources of insight.
- At each touchpoint, decide explicitly: automation (speed) or a human (empathy)? Most of the time, a combination.

## Example

Marriott's social listening center, M Live, spots a couple on honeymoon at one of the hotels and notifies that hotel, and the staff prepare a surprise. The machine detects, the human delights.
