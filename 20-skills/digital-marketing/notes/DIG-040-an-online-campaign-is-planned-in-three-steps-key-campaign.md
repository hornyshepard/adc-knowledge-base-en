# An online campaign is planned in three steps: key campaign decisions, sub-campaigns for each stage of the customer journey, monitoring and optimization

> **The idea in one sentence:** because the post-cookie era makes the contribution of each ad ever harder to prove, the authors structure the campaign by stages, each with its own measurable objectives.

## Explanation

**The preliminary considerations** (ch. 2.1): without third-party cookies, customer profiles get thinner; it becomes urgent for the company to build its own database; the contribution of ads to sales is ever harder to show, especially in the early stages. The consequences: customer knowledge becomes a key success factor, each stage gets a sub-campaign with its own objectives, and the big decisions are made at the start, so that the budget can be divided.

**Step 1: key campaign decisions**: the starting point and the objectives (the brief), buyer personas, the customer journey and the campaign groups, the budget.

**Step 2: the sub-campaigns for each stage**:

- *media planning*: mobile or desktop (mobile had about 61% of global advertising revenue in 2022), the online environment (search networks, publishers, blogs, social, programmatic, retargeting, comparison portals, aggregators, retailers, apps), the ad formats;
- the *design* of the advertising material;
- *booking*: directly with the publisher, through online marketers, through ad networks, programmatic or retargeting platforms, plus the quality settings.

**Step 3: success monitoring and optimization**: from metrics to KPIs for each stage, then refining the persona with the data on who responded.

## Application

- Use the three steps as the structure of the media plan or of a digital campaign pitch.
- Do not jump to choosing channels before the persona and the journey: the order matters, because the channels follow from them.
- Plan from step 1 how you will measure each stage.

## Example

The chapter's running example: a maker of AI-based video creation software for small and medium-sized businesses. It runs display for awareness, video testimonials on LinkedIn for appeal and a YouTube Video Action campaign for ask, each with different KPIs.
