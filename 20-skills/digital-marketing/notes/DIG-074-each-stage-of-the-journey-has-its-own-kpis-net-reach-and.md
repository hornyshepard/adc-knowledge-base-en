# Each stage of the journey has its own KPIs: net reach and frequency in Awareness, entry into the evoked set in Appeal, clicks and conversions in Ask, retention and CLV after purchase

> **The idea in one sentence:** if you judge every stage by sales, the early campaigns always look like failures; each stage has to be evaluated by its own objective.

## Explanation

For each subcampaign, Schwarz-Musch et al. choose three kinds of indicators: primary KPIs, tied to the objective, key metrics for measuring efficiency and metrics about user behavior.

| Stage | The person's state | Primary KPIs | Efficiency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | latent need, not yet searching | unique users (net reach), average contact frequency, impressions, CTR | CPM |
| **Appeal** | makes a pre-selection of brands | impressions, interactions, clicks, downloads | CPM, CPC |
| **Ask** | actively seeking information | clicks, CTR, purchases, conversion rate, ROI | cost per conversion |
| **After purchase** (Zahay et al.) | uses and re-evaluates | satisfaction, repeat purchases, CLV | |

The **evoked set** is the group of brands a person actively considers. The authors' rule: a brand that fails to anchor itself in the evoked set in the Appeal phase will not be considered in the rest of the purchase process. In Awareness, CTR alone gives an incomplete picture, because people may visit the website later, after seeing the ad (*post-view conversion*). In Ask you need conversion tracking on the website.

**Convergence.** Zahay et al. (fig. 5.6) make the same mapping over four stages: awareness, consideration, purchase and loyalty. Under consideration they list newsletter subscribers, repeat visits, comments and brand perceptions. The difference is one of scope. SRC-032 stops at Ask, because it deals with advertising before the purchase. Zahay et al. continue into the loyalty loop.

## Application

- In the media plan, give each stage a single primary KPI and at most two secondary ones.
- For Appeal, think of the creative work as a request to enter the evoked set: what should people remember about the brand?
- Explain to the client from the start why an Awareness campaign is not judged by sales.

## Example

The running example in SRC-032 is AI-based video creation software for small businesses. In Awareness it runs display, with an exposure limit of four contacts per person; a visit to the website is the sign of moving on. In Appeal it uses video testimonials on LinkedIn and downloadable case studies. In Ask, a YouTube Video Action Campaign drives people to the online store, with the conversion measured on the website.

## Questions

- How do you show a client that an Awareness campaign with a low CTR worked?
