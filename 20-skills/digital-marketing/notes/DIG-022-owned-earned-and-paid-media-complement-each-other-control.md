# Owned, earned and paid media complement each other: control, credibility and reach

> **The idea in one sentence:** owned media give you control, earned media give you credibility, paid media give you reach and speed; a complete strategy uses all three and makes them feed each other.

## Explanation

| Type | What it is | Advantage | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Owned** | the content you create and control: website, blog, social profiles, newsletter | complete control over the message and the timing | it is not free in practice: it takes human effort, and algorithms limit its reach |
| **Earned** | what others create about you: reviews, shares, mentions, articles, buzz | authenticity and trust (people trust peers more than brands) | the hardest and most time-consuming to generate |
| **Paid** | paid placement: PPC, display, retargeting, sponsored posts, paid influencers, traditional advertising | reach, frequency on demand, speed, control | it costs money and is viewed with distrust |

The authors stress that earned media are often the result of doing the first two well: a great Super Bowl ad is discussed and retweeted many times, and an entire industry has grown up around creating buzz for these ads. In content marketing, the recommended order is: begin with the brand's owned channels, get as much earned content as you can with reasonable effort, and fill out the picture with paid, to be sure you reach the target. Connors (SRC-030) describes the same triad as the distribution channels for content and points out that paid media work through targeting.

## Application

- For any idea, draw the route: where it is born (owned), what gets it shared (earned), how you speed it up (paid).
- Budget for "owned" as well: a website or a profile with no people to respond becomes an empty page.
- Measure separately: earned media cannot be bought, but they can be provoked through content and relationships.

## Example

Scotts Miracle-Gro invites its Facebook friends (social) to opt in to its newsletter (owned), where they get advice tailored to their grass type and climate zone: a chain in which each type of media feeds the next.

## Questions

- Is a paid influencer paid or earned media? Where do you draw the line?
- What does "shared media" mean (sometimes added as a fourth type), and why do the authors leave it out?
