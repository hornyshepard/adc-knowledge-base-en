# The Wheel of Social Media Engagement has five effects that drive it: information, connected, network, dynamic, timeliness

> **The idea in one sentence:** at the hub of the wheel is a repository of past and current engagements, and around it five effects specific to social media explain why people engage and how brands can keep the wheel turning.

## Explanation

Grewal and Levy propose the **Wheel of Social Media Engagement** (Exhibit 3.3):

| Effect | The mechanism | Example or figure from the book |
|---|---|---|
| Information | relevant information (funny, cute, instructive, surprising) is spread through the network; its relevance depends on the context and the receiver | Facebook Dynamic Ads target people who have already visited the retailer's website |
| Connected | it satisfies the innate need to connect; it works both ways (you learn and you pass on), and it brings social approval | Hallmark #CareEnough: you reflect on your loved ones and remember to buy a card |
| Network | each post reaches the connections of the person who posts it and spreads almost instantly; the influence depends on the credibility of those who share | CoverGirl has 600,000 followers, Katy Perry more than 100 million |
| Dynamic | two-way exchange solves problems fast; communities change as people come and go | a community about water bottles splits by interest (where to buy them, ecological impact) |
| Timeliness | engaging at the right place and time, 24/7; the benefit diminishes if you respond slowly | 80% expect a response to complaints within 24 hours, 64% on Twitter within 1 hour; the actual average: 1 day and 7 hours |

The authors' conclusion: firms invest not only in creating engagement but also in capturing engagement data. Posts are a rich source of information about customers.

## Application

- Design content around the effects: is it relevant enough to be passed on (information)? Does it feed a relationship (connected)? Can someone with a large network share it (network)?
- Organize the team for timeliness: a fast-response rule is part of the social media strategy.
- Watch how the community changes (the dynamic effect) and specialize content by sub-interest.

## Example

Coca-Cola used *beacon* technology to offer moviegoers a free Coke at the moment they walked into the theater (if they had the app). It is the timeliness effect applied at the point of purchase.

## Questions

- Is the "wheel" a tested theory or a teaching metaphor of the authors?
- Which effect is missing from the wheel for TikTok, where the algorithm, not the network of friends, decides the spread?
