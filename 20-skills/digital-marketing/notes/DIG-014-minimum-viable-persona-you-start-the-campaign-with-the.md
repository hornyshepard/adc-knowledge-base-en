# Minimum viable persona: you start the campaign with the minimum information needed and complete the persona from the campaign data

> **The idea in one sentence:** modeled on the minimum viable product, the minimum viable persona contains only what you need for the initial planning, and the campaigns enrich it along the way.

## Explanation

A complete persona requires long qualitative interviews. Digital campaigns, on the other hand, keep bringing in data about who responded. Hence the idea of the *minimum viable persona* (MVP), borrowed from the *minimum viable product* (Cooper and Vlaskovits, 2013).

In online advertising, the persona has three roles: to give insight into the message for each stage, to help select the touchpoints and to provide the targeting parameters. For this, the MVP has four blocks:

1. **Demographic data**: gender, age, region, job-related criteria (work experience, education), needed anyway in the platforms' settings. The authors ask for honesty: is the information reliable, or is it speculation?
2. **Interests**: targeting by interest is a unique feature of digital channels.
3. **The customer journey and the preferred digital channels**.
4. **Pain points and needs** at each stage, so you can formulate specific messages.

**Refinement** comes from the platforms' reports: Google shows age, gender, household income (where available) and parental status; LinkedIn shows job function, job title, industry, seniority, company size; Meta shows age, gender, region and the time the ad was seen; TikTok shows the top and bottom ten interests and the device.

## Application

- Write the MVP on a single page and mark the speculative fields in another color.
- After the first weeks of the campaign, update the persona with the audience actually reached and compare it with the hypotheses.
- Do not wait for the "perfect" persona to launch; you are waiting for data that only the launch can give you.

## Example

The authors return throughout to a maker of AI-powered video creation software for SMEs: it starts with a basic persona (the owner of a small business, with no budget for a video agency), then learns from the LinkedIn reports which job functions and industries actually interacted with the testimonial videos.

## Questions

- The reports show whom the algorithm chose to show the ad to, not necessarily your market. How do you avoid a persona that only confirms the algorithm?
