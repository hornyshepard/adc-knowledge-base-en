# Each buyer persona gets its own campaign group, split into sub-campaigns for Aware, Appeal and Ask, each with separate objectives and KPIs

> **The idea in one sentence:** a single campaign for all personas lumps them into one nameless mass; one group per persona allows different messages, channels and objectives for each stage.

## Explanation

The authors start from two observations: it is difficult to reach different personas with one campaign, because you cannot tailor the message to the pain points of each; and at each stage a different sub-goal has to be achieved, so that the persona keeps considering you in the next stage. Their rule: define a campaign group for each persona; each group has sub-campaigns for the pre-buy stages (Aware, Appeal, Ask); each sub-campaign has its own objectives and KPIs.

## Application

- Build a persona × stage matrix: objective, message, channel, payment model, KPI.
- Check whether the ad's first "job" is to sell the category or the brand; the answer differs from persona to persona.
- The same product may need two different creative strategies.

## Example

A manufacturer launches an e-mountain bike and has two personas.

| Stage | Persona 1: first-time buyer (rides a conventional mountain bike, knows nothing about e-bikes) | Persona 2: experienced rider of a competitor's e-bike |
|---|---|---|
| Aware | the benefits of the category first (uphill rides are less strenuous, so you ride more); Google Display Network, YouTube, Facebook, because the persona is not searching yet | brand awareness only; search engine ads, because the persona is already searching |
| Appeal | the brand image through short, emotional videos on Instagram and YouTube | also emotional videos, adapted to other motivations |
| Ask | searches for product features: Google Ads and YouTube | expert searches, hence different keywords |

## Questions

- How many personas can a small client's budget support before each group becomes too thin?
- How do you keep a single brand idea when you have six different sub-campaigns?
