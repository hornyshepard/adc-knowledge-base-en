# YouTube video formats are chosen by objective: masthead and 6-second bumper for reach, TrueView for consideration, Video Action for conversion

> **The idea in one sentence:** each video format has a different objective, a different payment model and different length limits, and therefore a different creative requirement.

## Explanation

The table in the book (Germany, second half of 2022):

| Marketing objective | Video solution | What it is | Bids and prices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach and brand awareness | Video Masthead | space on the YouTube home page and in the home feed | fixed CPM of €5.20 + €0.80 targeting surcharge |
| | Bumper Ads | non-skippable 6-second video | €3.00 to €5.50 CPM |
| | TrueView for Reach | skippable video, unlimited length | €0.01 to €0.04 CPV (cost per view) |
| Consideration | TrueView In-Stream | skippable; payment is only required when users watch or interact with the ad | €0.01 to €0.04 CPV |
| | In-Feed Video for Consideration | click-to-play video | €0.04 to €0.09 CPV |
| Action | Video Action Campaign | skippable video ads that promote lower-funnel actions (website clicks) | target CPA, max conversions |

The goals in Google Ads for YouTube include: "Efficient reach" (a combination of bumper, skippable in-stream, in-feed and Shorts ads), "Target Frequency," "Non-skippable reach" (up to 15 seconds), "Drive conversions," "Ad sequence" (a narrative told through ads shown in order to the same viewer) and "Audio." Skippable ads can typically be skipped after 5 seconds or less.

The success factors of YouTube videos, according to the studies cited: authenticity, innovation, interactivity.

## Application

- The 6-second bumper calls for a single idea, with no introduction.
- In skippable formats, the hook and the brand have to appear in the first 5 seconds.
- An ad sequence allows storytelling in episodes: plan the narrative arc before you shoot.

## Example

For the maker of AI-powered video creation software in the book, the ask phase uses a Video Action Campaign that drives people straight to the online store, with the conversion measured on the website, not a reach format.

## Questions

- Can a creative idea work equally well in 6, 15 and 60 seconds? What has to stay constant?
