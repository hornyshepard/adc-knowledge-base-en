# The 7A's of digital marketing (Add, Allure, Advance, Adapt, Associate, Abide, Analyze) complement the 4P's

> **The idea in one sentence:** Sachdev proposes a "digital marketing mix" made of seven activities that start with A, used alongside the 4P's, not instead of them.

## Explanation

Raj Sachdev considers that McCarthy's 4P's (1960) still apply, but they do not describe what a marketer actually does in the digital environment. So he proposes the 7A's, also called the digital marketing mix:

| A | What it means | The check question |
|---|---|---|
| **Add** | value for the consumer, the organization and society; a measurable return | Do we communicate the value? Do we add something extra? |
| **Allure** | any experience that attracts or fascinates and entices toward an action: promotions, information, storytelling | Is the content creative, informative, attractive? |
| **Advance** | you help people move from one step to the next toward conversion | Clear menu, search, a visible CTA, chat? |
| **Adapt/Adjust** | you adjust quickly to the environment and to behavior | What do we change if we miss the target? |
| **Associate** | brand association, brand affiliation, strategic relationships with partners and influencers | Do the logo, the colors and the symbols support the association? |
| **Abide** | customer preferences, the law, ethics, privacy, security | Do we comply with the advertising and data rules? |
| **Analyze** | monitoring, measurement, metrics, insight | Do we act on what we report? |

The author stresses that not every A gets the same focus: the mix is chosen according to the strategy. In the strategic plan, the 4P's and the 7A's appear together in the strategies and tactics section.

## Application

- Use the table as a quick audit: score each A from 1 to 5 for a campaign and look for the weak link.
- For the strategist: *Advance* is where money gets lost, for example a click on a product ad that lands on the homepage.
- For the creative: *Allure* and *Associate* are the "creative" A's; the others are the conditions under which the idea can work.

## Example

The book applies the model to Apple: valuable technology (Add), launches that fascinate (Allure), digital support throughout (Advance), adapting to technology and needs (Adapt), affiliation and loyalty (Associate), respecting privacy options (Abide), insight from analytics (Analyze). Home Depot illustrates *Advance*: a clear menu, categories, "Shop Now" buttons, chat.

## Questions

- What do the 7A's add to AIDA + 4P's? *Allure* resembles attention and interest, *Advance* desire and action.
- Is the model a theory or only a checklist? The book does not validate it empirically.
