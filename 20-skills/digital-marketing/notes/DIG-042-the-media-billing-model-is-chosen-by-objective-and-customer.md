# The media billing model is chosen by objective and customer journey stage: CPM for awareness, CPV/CPE for appeal, CPC/CPL for ask, CPA for purchase

> **The idea in one sentence:** the billing model is not a technical detail; it says what you are actually buying, and it has to match what you want to achieve at that stage.

## Explanation

Schwarz-Musch and colleagues tie the billing models to the stages of the journey:

| Stage | Model | You pay for |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | CPM (cost per mille), CPE (cost per engagement) | a thousand impressions; an interaction |
| Appeal | CPV (cost per view), CPE | a video view; an interaction |
| Ask | CPC (cost per click), CPL (cost per lead) | a click; a lead (for example, a newsletter sign-up) |
| Purchase | CPA (cost per acquisition) | a sale or a registration |

Sachdev makes the same link for SEM: clicks → CPC; brand visibility → vCPM (cost per thousand viewable impressions); conversions → CPA; views → CPV. CPM is calculated as the price of the ad divided by reach, times 1,000, which makes it possible to compare media.

The American textbook adds the distinction between **manual bidding** (you set the maximum CPC when you know the industry's costs), **automated bidding** and **smart bidding** (machine learning that adjusts the bid to maximize conversions within the budget).

## Application

- In the media plan, put on the same row: the stage, the objective, the billing model, the KPI.
- Do not buy CPC for an image campaign, and do not judge a CPM campaign by immediate sales.
- Paying for verified sales also protects against fraud, but it makes sense only when the objective is the sale itself.

## Example

Sachdev shows why it matters: at a CPC of $3.25, 20 clicks cost $65; if only one person buys a product priced at $4.99, the campaign loses money. On YouTube (Germany, second half of 2022), the Video Masthead sold at a fixed CPM of €5.20, bumper ads at €3.00 to €5.50 CPM, and TrueView formats at €0.01 to €0.04 per view.

## Questions

- What incentives does each model give the platform? Who bears the risk in a CPM deal, and who in a CPA deal?
- Why can a low CTR be acceptable in a campaign paid on CPM?
