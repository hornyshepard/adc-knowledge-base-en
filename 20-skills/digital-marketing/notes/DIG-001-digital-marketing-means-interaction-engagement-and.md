# Digital marketing means interaction, engagement and measurement, not only new channels

> **The idea in one sentence:** digital marketing is using any digital technology to facilitate the marketing process, with the end goal of customer interaction, engagement and measurement, a direct legacy of direct marketing.

## Explanation

Debra Zahay offers a "dynamic" definition rather than a list of channels: what counts is the end goal (interaction, engagement, measurement), not the technology. The definition comes from **direct marketing**. The DMA required a direct program to be an organized and planned system of contacts, using a variety of media, seeking to produce a lead or an order, built on a database and measurable in cost and results. In short: any system that is based on data analysis, has a clear objective and is measurable is direct marketing. In the past only catalog senders (Lands' End) had data; today everyone has it, so we speak of data-driven marketing.

Why is the internet "the ultimate direct response medium"? The authors describe it through the **four Is**:

| I | What it allows |
|---|---|
| Interactive | two-way dialog, offers changed based on the response |
| Information-driven | every click becomes data for targeting |
| Immediate | fast response, event-triggered offers |
| Involving | video, games, rich media that draw participation |

The practical consequence: the more third-party data disappears, the more first-party data collected from websites, apps and customer databases matters.

## Application

- When you receive a "digital" brief, ask: what interaction do we want, what engagement, and how do we measure it? If it cannot be measured, it is not yet a digital strategy.
- Check whether the creative idea uses at least one of the four Is; a banner that only "displays" wastes the medium.
- Think of every execution as a response mechanism: what does the person do after seeing the message?

## Example

Amazon is the authors' example: someone who buys a book for left-handed golfers is shown other products for left-handed individuals, through collaborative filtering. Purchase data feeds the next offer directly, without mail, phone or direct response television.

## Questions

- If the definition puts measurement at the center, what happens to brand effects that are hard to measure in the short term?
- Is a YouTube video ad without a CTA "digital marketing" in Zahay's sense?
