# Digital readiness is assessed on two axes, customers ready to migrate and a capable company: four quadrants, each with its own strategy

> **The idea in one sentence:** there is no single digital strategy; first you check whether the market wants to go digital and whether the firm can, then you choose between migrating customers, building capabilities or strengthening your leadership.

## Explanation

Marketing 5.0 treats the COVID-19 pandemic as an accelerator of digitalization: deliveries, digital banking, videoconferencing, telehealth. The crisis showed who was ready. The diagnosis crosses the **demand side** (are customers ready to migrate to digital touchpoints?) with the **supply side** (can the company digitalize its business processes?). The result is four quadrants (Fig. 5.3, with U.S. examples):

| Quadrant | Customers | Company | Example | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Origin** | not very ready | not very ready | hospitality, healthcare (heavy human-to-human interaction) | both migration and capabilities |
| **Onward** | not very ready | ready | grocery retailing (e-commerce slightly less than 12% of retail trade in Q1 2020) | **migrate customers** |
| **Organic** | ready | not very ready | automotive: 95% use digital as the primary source of information, more than 95% still buy at the dealership | **build capabilities** |
| **Omni** | ready | ready | high-tech, financial services | **strengthen digital leadership** |

**Migrating customers** (Origin, Onward): positive or negative incentives (cashback, or additional charges for the offline option), addressing frustration points (queues, complicated processes, human error) and recreating desired physical interactions with digital (video banking, chatbots).

**Building capabilities** (Origin, Organic): customer data infrastructure and business processes reengineered, not merely automated; digital customer experience across all touchpoints and new business models (subscription, marketplace, on-demand); a strong digital organization (talent such as data scientists and UX designers, an agile culture).

**Strengthening leadership** (Omni): adopt the next tech (content marketing and e-commerce are already hygiene factors), introduce **the new CX** at three levels (informative, interactive, immersive) and strengthen the position as a **digital-first brand**: vision, assets and people put first at the service of the digital customer.

## Application

- At the start of a digital project, place the agency's client in a quadrant, using data on consumer behavior and an internal audit.
- For a client in Onward, the campaign has to motivate migration (a clear benefit, an incentive), not only announce the app.
- For a client in Organic, the problem is not communication but internal capability. Say so in the pitch.

## Example

Bangalore airport introduced a "parking-to-boarding" contactless experience, and some restaurants switched to cloud or ghost kitchens serving only delivery orders. These are responses to the pressure of the pandemic in industries from the Origin quadrant.

## Questions

- The industry positions are for the United States in 2020. Where would the same industries sit in Romania today?
- The book assumes digital is always the destination. Are there categories where migration is not worth it?
