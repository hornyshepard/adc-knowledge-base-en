# Digital and performance marketing (DIG)

## What mastering this skill means

Observable competencies ("you can…"):

- **C1** You can formulate a digital strategy that starts from the business: you choose the generic strategy (acquisition, conversion, retention, value growth), write the chain marketing objective → digital objective → value for the customer → metric and the digital value proposition, then set them out in a structured plan.
- **C2** You can build the persona and the customer journey of a campaign (trigger, expectations, barriers, influences, touchpoints, involvement) and mark the friction points and the breaks between channels.
- **C3** You can audit a brand's owned media: the website (process, conventions, UX, accessibility), SEO (intent, pillars), email (permission, offer, metrics) and the relationship after purchase (CRM, apps, community).
- **C4** You can plan a paid campaign by persona and stage: you choose the channel, the format and the billing model, write congruent ads and landing pages, and set brand safety, viewability, the frequency cap and fraud protection.
- **C5** You can explain what changed with the end of third-party cookies and propose targeting and data without them: context and semantics, prediction, first-party data with valid consent, clean rooms.
- **C6** You can check a campaign before launch for privacy, consent, transparency (native advertising, influencers), copyright and trust.
- **C7** You can choose the KPIs by objective and by stage, read a conversion funnel, design a correct A/B test and discuss the limits of attribution honestly.
- **C8** You can show the client where the authors disagree (the offer vs. the creative, the funnel vs. the loop, labeled vs. ambiguous native, last-click vs. multi-touch) and what that means for the budget.
